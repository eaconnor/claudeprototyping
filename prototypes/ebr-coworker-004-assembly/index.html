Onboarding and configuration used to be two artifacts. They described the same thing twice, so they were merged. The configuration — primitives, the dual-surface split, the intent-signal map, and the two output surfaces — now lives at step 4 of onboarding, presented as inferences drawn from the operator's own report rather than a form to fill in. This page used to hold that content; it's a router now.
What is fabricated, in both surfaces: every grade and every confidence tier. No model runs anywhere in here — the interpretation layer is the actual product and it is the one thing these prototypes hollow out. The pulls, the schema mismatches, the entity-resolution failures, the gates and the state machine are all real.

Open, and load-bearing

· The EBR Review primitive is scaffolded, not confirmed — every surface depends on it structurally.
· The "8" is unreconciled: at least four non-equivalent 8s live in the corpus (tabs, connectors, sources, integrations). These artifacts say "8 tabs" throughout.
· The practitioner's real tabs, opening move, and gap-logic are ⟨VERIFY⟩ in the runbook and encoded here as if settled.
· Risk tier (high vs medium) is unconfirmed, so the sign-off gate runs on an assumption nobody ratified.
· usecure has no MCP server and no confirmed public API — it arrives by hand, and the report covers it anyway.
