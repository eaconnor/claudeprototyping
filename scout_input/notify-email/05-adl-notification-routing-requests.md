## ADL-1947 — Custom Email Routing for Custom Detection Notifications
Sub-task · Open · Reporter/Assignee: Carla Gajdecki · https://n-able.atlassian.net/browse/ADL-1947

**Problem (verbatim):**
> Today, custom detections rely on profile-based detection recipients, which limits customer flexibility. Customers like **Magna5** build custom detections knowing the SOC will not act on them, but lack a clean way to route notifications to a dedicated email inbox or team. This creates operational friction and makes it harder for customers to efficiently own and respond to their custom detection signals.

- Requested by: **Magna5**
- Must-have includes: throttle to prevent alert flooding; alerting if email routing fails; baseline notification volume at release.
- Prioritization score: **1.95** (Revenue Low, Strategic Medium, Customer Demand Medium, DIR High).

## ADL-1446 — Team-Based Notification Routing for Jira Ticket Alerts
Sub-task · Open · Reporter/Assignee: Carla Gajdecki · https://n-able.atlassian.net/browse/ADL-1446

**Problem (verbatim):**
> Currently, every Jira ticket generated by Adlumin is sent to all assigned users or teams, regardless of the alert type or relevance to their role. There is no way to route notifications to specific teams based on the nature of the alert (e.g., database, firewall, endpoint). This results in unnecessary notification noise, slower response times, and increased risk of missed or ignored critical alerts.

- Request originator: **Jared Dewitt**
- Outcome: team-targeted alert routing; reduce alert fatigue.

## ADL-1087 — SOC Ticket Number Visibility in UI and Notifications
Sub-task · Open · Reporter/Assignee: Carla Gajdecki · https://n-able.atlassian.net/browse/ADL-1087

**Problem (verbatim):**
> When partners receive email notifications such as "SOC-395163 Samson Electric Inc - The security operations team has requested customer review on a medium severity firewall log reduction alarm," the **SOC-# is the unique identifier** for the event. However, in the Adlumin partner portal, users must open each event and scroll to the bottom of the notes to find the SOC-######.

- Requested by: **Fairbanks Tech Support (2025)**
- Must-have: include the SOC ticket number in all related email notifications; make it filterable/searchable.
- **Direct relevance to NOT-4 templates:** the email body must surface the SOC-# / Jira ticket link prominently — this request is exactly about the email template content.

## ADL-1062 — Custom Notification Routing for Custom Detections
Sub-task · Open · Reporter/Assignee: Carla Gajdecki · https://n-able.atlassian.net/browse/ADL-1062

**Problem (verbatim):**
> Currently, users can create custom detections … but cannot specify who should receive the notification when the alert is triggered. The only routing option available is to send alerts to the SOC team. There is no field or mechanism to input specific email addresses.

- Requested by: **Integris** (targeting Q1 2026)
- Nice-to-have: "SMS or phone call routing for critical alerts" — foreshadows the Twilio channel.

## Ingredients for the prototype
- Real customer names (use as evidence, NOT as seed data): Magna5, Integris, Fairbanks Tech Support, Jared Dewitt, Samson Electric Inc.
- The recurring theme: **right alert → right recipient → with enough context to act.** NOT-4's per-profile templates + Jira link + recommended actions is the engine these requests need.
- "Profile-based detection recipients" is the existing routing primitive the email service resolves against.
