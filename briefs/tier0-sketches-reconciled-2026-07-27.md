## Sketch A · "A Head Nerd By Your Side" — the kitted Client-Facing Proof *(lead)*

1. **Problem / wicked bit:** cross-vendor client-ready proof-of-value is unsolved ("the holy grail — nobody has it," MSP-A `[CS: HIGH]`), and advisory is a **job shop** the market keeps trying to automate into commodity.
2. **Tension (Kolko):** MSPs are trying to prove strategic value to the client every quarter, but the evidence is scattered across 6+ tools *and* the real job — a forward-looking executive conversation — needs judgment they can't scale, so they hand-build a report or hand it to generic AI that drafts a commodity version.
3. **How might we:** give an MSP a **Head-Nerd-caliber advisor by their side** who turns their own telemetry into a client-ready, defensible, forward-looking review — without the MSP having to *be* that expert?
4. **Value prop (Moore):** For N-able-stack MSP owners who must prove value and grow the account, *A Head Nerd By Your Side* is a kitted advisory coworker that drafts a defensibility-tagged EBR from first-party telemetry and coaches the forward-looking conversation. Unlike report generators (ScalePad) or generic AI, it carries the codified Head Nerd corpus and is **used-and-tested by the Head Nerds themselves.**
5. **TAM:** parent = vCISO/vCIO share-of-services ~$1.4–4.3B `[CS: MEDIUM]`.
6. **Necessary functionality:** QBR/EBR runbook on the existing engine · **Value Ledger producer wired** (renders enterprise value, not $0) · Defensibility tiering (M/B/E/U; headline Measured only) · Stefanie-methodology schema (risk register → pipeline) · human 15-min edit-and-send.
7. **Evidence + [CS:]:** QBR #1 across all instruments `[CS: HIGH / R+D]`; report-framing is a misfit `[R internal SME]`; Value Ledger built but renders $0 `[CS: VERIFIED]`; "used and tested" trust `[A]`. Strength: **STRONG demand · A on the mechanism.**

9. **N-able superpower:** first-party full-stack telemetry + SOC 90% auto-remediation corpus + the Head Nerd corpus/brand.

## Sketch B · "The Seat That Becomes The Flagship" — the governed bundle

*Reconciles the seat-vs-flagship tension: B is the priced **seat** under A/C, **and** the **flagship** when the whole bundle is sold as governed-and-auditable. The board deck's ~5% error was pitching it undifferentiated ("system IT runs on") with no moat.*

1. **Problem / wicked bit:** governing agent sprawl + accountability of non-human actors — *"AIs can't be fired, sued, taken to court… there has to be a human in the loop"* (Derek `[CS: HIGH]`). Governance-as-checkbox is table-stakes (8/10 competitors); **decision-quality on owned telemetry** is not.
2. **Tension (Kolko):** MSPs want to hand real work to agents but can't put their name behind an actor they can't govern or audit — so they avoid agents or drown in sprawl; *and* if N-able's plane is closed, a mixed-stack MSP needs two planes.
3. **How might we:** make N-able's control plane the one an MSP wants across *every* vendor's agents — by winning on decision quality (owned telemetry), not protocol lock-in?
4. **Value prop (Moore):** For MSPs adopting agents they must stand behind, B is a governed, auditable, MCP/A2A-open control plane riding N-able's owned telemetry. Unlike a closed plane or a governance point-tool, it makes the whole coworker bundle governable-and-billable and plugs into whatever plane the MSP already runs.
5. **TAM:** standalone ~$3B midpoint but **no per-seat precedent** and line-item WTP unproven `[CS: MEDIUM / WOBBLY]`; realistic value = the seat + the bundle's governability.
6. **Necessary functionality:** Runbook Engine (built) · autonomy tiers (ratified) · Value Ledger metering · **behavioral-conformance mechanism + Activity Ledger** (the "prove we operate it" build) · MCP/A2A compat · SPIFFE/SPIRE for N-able's own fleet.

9. **N-able superpower:** Deterministic Substitution Engine + first-party stack as the governed substrate + installed base as the *governor-of-all-agents* position.
