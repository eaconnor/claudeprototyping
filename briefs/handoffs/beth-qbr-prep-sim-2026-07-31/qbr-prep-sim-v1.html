Your practice handles client money, payroll records, and personal financial information every working day. Decisions we need from you

11 staff accounts can currently be opened with a password alone. For a practice holding client financial records, a single guessed or stolen password is the difference between business as usual and telling clients their information was exposed. We recommend adding a second sign-in step for these accounts before your busy season starts — the work is routine and staff disruption is minimal.

evidence: verified

Your agreement with us includes managed threat detection on every device. Nine of your 61 devices are not yet covered — most are recent starter laptops in the York office. evidence: verified

Four devices are behind on important updates. Out-of-date machines are the most common way problems get in, and these four sit in the payroll workflow. We recommend scheduling the updates outside working hours this month.

evidence: verified against our records; comparison context estimated

4 · Refresh awareness training for the busy season

The attempts we stopped this period were increasingly aimed at your accounts team by name. Based on what we saw, we judge your staff's exposure to convincing fake emails to be elevated, and we recommend a short refresher before January. This recommendation is our professional reading of an incomplete picture — our testing platform was interrupted mid-period — and we'd rather tell you that plainly than present it as a measurement.

evidence: estimated
