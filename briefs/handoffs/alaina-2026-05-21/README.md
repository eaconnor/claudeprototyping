## Quick context

This is a test of a new design protocol. The prototype is the Tier-1 visual; the rail is the diagnostic. Together they sit *before* Nicole Reineke's 13-point Readiness Gate as the pre-gate coaching loop. Bradley is the n-able-PM-coordinator persona that convenes a "council of methods" (JTBD, Service Blueprint, SDT, Porter, Christensen, etc.) and reports back to the PM in plain language with concrete consequences for skipping research or design work.

The bet: if we make design and research risks visible *and consequential* at prototype time, PMs route to you and Julian earlier, not after the wrong thing's been built.
