Reading this the agile way: commit to Now, shape Next, hold Later loosely. Items move left as they earn confidence, and the workstream colors let you follow one theme end to end. Ticket chips link the related Jira initiatives and epics per horizon; some (Apex, the onboarding wizard, granular RBAC) recur across columns because the initiative spans horizons — see the Threads panel above for the full multi-step view. Dates are directional, since these initiatives have no fixed release versions yet. The shared Catalog + SOAR pattern framework sits in Now on purpose so Next and Later inherit patterns rather than reinvent them.

On the Problem / $ Upside additions: every claim tagged D is quoted from a number the linked Jira ticket itself states — I did not calculate or invent any figure. Claims tagged A are my inference from the ticket's framing, not a stated fact — treat them as hypotheses for Beth, Meaghan, and Nicole to validate, not settled numbers.
