The Story

What happened
Between 14:02 and 14:21 EST, the user user.acme@corp authenticated from an unusual ASN, escalated privileges on host-east-04, then attempted lateral movement to host-east-09. Microsoft Defender flagged the second host; Adlumin SOAR isolated it within 38 seconds.
What we did
Three detections from two sources (Azure AD anomalous logon + Defender malicious activity + Adlumin SOAR isolation echo) were consolidated into this incident. Without Alpha, this would have generated four separate tickets. The isolation suppression rule fired correctly — notification suppressed · consolidated
What to do next
Confirm whether user.acme@corp is a known traveller before unlocking. Pull the Defender quarantine report. If unfamiliar geography persists, escalate to customer Tier-2 with the consolidated incident timeline. Estimated time to close: 8 min.
