## 1. ACTIVITIES — Typical agenda / running order

### 1a. A prescriptive 60-minute running order exists in the community
Sequence: 0:00–0:08 Delivery Review · 0:08–0:20 Security/Infrastructure Gap Report · 0:20–0:45 12-Month Opportunity Slate · 0:45–0:55 Single Decision Ask · 0:55–1:00 Next Steps + confirm next QBR date. Opportunity slate = the "critical middle section (25 minutes)... the section most MSPs skip."
[CS: MEDIUM] [WOBBLY] — single vendor/community playbook, prescriptive not observational.
https://msprospector.com/blog/msp-qbr-playbook

### 1b. Alternate 7-component agenda (technical-heavy)
Service Ticket Review → Technical Review ("often makes up the bulk of your business review process") → SLA Review → User Training → Strategic Planning → Endpoint Management → Network Security Defense.
[CS: MEDIUM] [WOBBLY] — vendor blog (N-able's OWN published framing).
https://www.n-able.com/blog/msp-quarterly-business-review

### 1c. Generic flow: review → alignment → decision → confirmation
Begins with structured KPI review tied to prior quarter's goals; go beyond numbers to the "why."
[CS: MEDIUM] [WOBBLY]
https://jumpcloud.com/blog/msp-quarterly-review-guide

### 1d. Where "growth opportunities" land: the middle, framed as a scored/priced slate
Gap report built, opportunity categories scored, account manager prices top 3–5 items against the line card. Growth is a structured mid-meeting slate, not an ad-hoc mention.
[CS: MEDIUM] [WOBBLY]
https://msprospector.com/blog/msp-qbr-playbook

### 1e. Where "next steps" land: last 5 minutes + a single decision ask
Ends with one commitment request on the top opportunity; success = "a verbal yes or surfacing the real objection," then confirm next QBR date.
[CS: MEDIUM] [WOBBLY]
https://msprospector.com/blog/msp-qbr-playbook

### 1f. Cadence
"Quarterly" (90 days) default. Dissent: cadence should follow client need (six months / monthly / weekly).
[CS: MEDIUM] [WOBBLY]
https://jumpcloud.com/blog/msp-quarterly-review-guide · https://startgrowmanage.com/better-than-a-qbr-rethinking-your-strategic-business-review/

### 1g. Duration
Range 1–2 hours; 2 hours cited as in-person benchmark; some run under an hour with automated prep; one vendor claims a "20 minute" template.
[CS: MEDIUM] [WOBBLY]
https://scopable.io/blog/msp-qbr-template · https://convergencenetworks.com/faqs/what-is-a-quarterly-business-review-qbr/
