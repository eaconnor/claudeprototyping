## The claim (verbatim substance)
Reframes the QBR from a check-the-box exercise into a **TEST** of two things:
1. **The customer's commitment to the partnership** — as a proxy for *"does the customer still care about solving this problem?"*
2. **Your (the AM's) ability to get the right people to the meeting** — as a proxy for *"do you have this account under control?"*

Consequence he draws: the whole approach to the QBR changes — you pay close attention to *the answers to those tests* (attendance, engagement) as signal. Frames it as "scary and awkward."

Claims top AMs already think this way and deliver **95%+ GRR, 120%+ NRR, and are "generally impossible to surprise."**
