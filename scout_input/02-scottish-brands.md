## Tunnock's Product Line

**Company:** Thomas Tunnock Limited, founded 1890, based in Uddingston, Scotland [CS: VERIFIED]
**Cultural status:** "Iconic Scottish brand" — packaging features Scottish lion rampant [CS: HIGH]

### Full Product Lineup

1. **Caramel Wafer Biscuit** (standard size) — Five layers of wafer separated by four layers of caramel, coated in chocolate [CS: VERIFIED]
2. **Mini Caramel Wafer Tub** (bite-sized version)
3. **Teacakes** — Developed 1956 by Sir Boyd Tunnock. Marshmallow center on biscuit base, encased in milk chocolate [CS: VERIFIED]
4. **Wafer Cream Biscuit**
5. **Snowballs** — Similar to Tea Cake with added grated coconut, no biscuit base [CS: VERIFIED]
6. **Caramel Log Biscuits** (standard size)
7. **Mini Caramel Log** (smaller version)

## Irn-Bru

**Company:** A.G. Barr (Scottish company)
**Cultural status:** "Scotland's other national drink" (after whisky) [CS: HIGH]
**Market position:** "Most popularly consumed soft drink in Scotland, consistently beating Coca-Cola, Pepsi and Fanta" [CS: HIGH]
**Sales:** "Reportedly sells 20 cans every second throughout Scotland" [CS: MEDIUM — no primary source cited]

### Vending Machine Presence in Scotland

[CS: HIGH] Multiple sources confirm widespread availability:
- "In Scotland, Irn-Bru is everywhere—it's in corner stores, vending machines, and every fridge from Glasgow to Inverness" (irnbru.us)
- "Coca Cola and Irn Bru, plus their Diet versions, still dominate the cold drinks vending market" in Scotland (vendingmachineglasgow.org)
- Photographic evidence of Irn-Bru vending machines at Edinburgh Airport, Glasgow Airport, Aberdeen Airport
