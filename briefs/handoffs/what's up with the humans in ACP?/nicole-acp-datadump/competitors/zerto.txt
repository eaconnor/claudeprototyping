## Overview
Zerto is a continuous data protection (CDP) and disaster recovery platform acquired by Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE) for $374M in 2021. Zerto differentiates through near-zero RPO/RTO — protecting workloads with second-by-second replication rather than periodic snapshots — enabling recovery to any point in time before a ransomware attack or failure. Integrated into the HPE GreenLake cloud services portfolio, Zerto serves 9,000+ customers including 350+ MSPs delivering DRaaS.

## MSP Relevance
Zerto is relevant to MSPs delivering enterprise-grade disaster recovery and business continuity services for clients with stringent recovery SLAs. Its CDP model (continuous, real-time replication vs. scheduled snapshots) enables MSPs to offer sub-minute RPO guarantees — a capability no snapshot-based backup product can match. Zerto's HPE partnership and 350+ MSP customer base provide a structured channel with volume-based DRaaS pricing. MSPs serving financial services, healthcare, and manufacturing clients with zero-downtime requirements are the primary Zerto audience.

## Key Offerings & Capabilities
- Continuous Data Protection (CDP) — real-time block-level replication with no data loss window (RPO in seconds)
- Near-zero RTO disaster recovery — recovery to any recent point in time with rapid failover
- Workload mobility — live migration across on-premises, Azure, AWS, and VMware environments
- Ransomware recovery — recover to a clean point in time before infection; Integration Hub with CrowdStrike for threat-triggered recovery (Q4 2025)
- HPE GreenLake integration — Zerto available as managed service within HPE's cloud portfolio
- Integration Hub — extensible platform enabling third-party security tools to trigger Zerto recovery actions (launched Q4 2025)
- Microsoft Defender integration — real-time threat visibility with automated recovery post-detection (Q2 2026)
- AI-assisted CDP — AI enhancements for continuous data protection (Q2 2026 HPE GreenLake update)
- DRaaS — MSPs offer flexible billing (pay-as-you-go, reserved, dedicated resources) for managed disaster recovery
- Per-VM licensing — Zerto Essential CloudEdition (ECE) and Advanced Resilience Edition (ARE)

## Pricing Model
Per-VM subscription licensing (not per-site or per-capacity). Two editions: Zerto Essential CloudEdition (ECE) and Zerto Advanced Resilience Edition (ARE). Starting approximately $745/year per VM; minimum deal of 15 VMs (~$15,000/year). Annual or multi-year terms. Pricing described as "complex and costly" in market reviews. All transactions handled through channel partners — no direct sales model. Custom enterprise pricing negotiated through HPE and authorized resellers.

## Strengths
- Continuous replication (CDP) is a genuine technical differentiator — the only enterprise platform offering second-by-second recovery point across entire workloads
- Integration Hub + CrowdStrike partnership is the most technically advanced ransomware recovery capability in the market
- 9,000+ customers including 350+ MSPs validates production-scale deployment at significant breadth
- VMware-to-HPE migration path is strategically positioned for Broadcom pricing disruption — MSPs with VMware clients have a new reason to evaluate Zerto
- AI-assisted CDP (Q2 2026) adds intelligent automation to the world's most granular recovery technology

## Weaknesses / Gaps
- Per-VM pricing at $745+/year creates high cost floor; minimum 15 VM deal (~$15K/year) excludes smaller MSP clients
- Pricing complexity — multiple editions, per-VM tiers, and volume discounts make proposals difficult to construct quickly
- HPE ownership creates enterprise-focused sales motion that may deprioritize SMB-focused MSPs
- Continuous replication is compute-intensive — requires more infrastructure resources than snapshot-based backup
- Not a SaaS product — requires significant infrastructure commitment on both MSP and client sides

## Competitive Threat to NZO
Level: Low
Reason: Zerto is a disaster recovery platform with no meaningful overlap with NZO's AI agent and automation capabilities. As a backup/DR vendor, Zerto occupies a distinct market segment from NZO. The only integration opportunity is NZO agents triggering Zerto recovery actions through the Integration Hub API model — which is a partnership opportunity rather than a competitive conflict.
