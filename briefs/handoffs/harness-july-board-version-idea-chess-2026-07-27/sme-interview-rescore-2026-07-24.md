## Headline: the evidence reshapes the set, it doesn't just score it

The domain expert attacked the #1 concept's premise. Read together, three SME threads converge:

- **QBR-as-report is misframed.** Stefanie (raw 1:10:45, said twice): MSPs ask for a QBR runbook "because they're taking it from a technical standpoint." Lewis (raw 48:22): "**We do not enable QBRs. - **Generation is commoditized; data is a short-lived moat.** Lewis (raw 58:06): "**Asking Claude is the new starting point.**" The durable moat is "a curated, carefully orchestrated **journey** you're intelligently guided through" + the codified expert corpus.
- **The deepest buyer want is liability transfer — which none of the concepts deliver.** Nicole (raw 3:32/3:37): "**They want a transfer of liability. They're okay with it being used as long as *we're* the liable ones.**" Defensibility/Black Box *document and tier* claims; they do not transfer liability.
