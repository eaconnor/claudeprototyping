# Council Testimony — ACP Nine Bet-Level Themes
**Instrument:** acp-nicole-evidence-crosswalk.md (built 2026-09-02)
**Task:** size the holes, characterize the gaps — not re-find them. Testimony, not synthesis. Contradictions preserved, not resolved.

Hole-size and gap-nature classifications are Claude's deductive read against each lens's own criteria (§18) — not new field findings. Every evidentiary claim keeps its crosswalk EV-AB/EV-AX/EV-WM/U-EV citation.

### Jobs-to-Be-Done (Christensen/Ulwick) — the crosswalk shows a job that hasn't been named, only ranked

**T1 · Real demand.** Hole: **large**. Nature: **knowledge-gap + framing-error** stacked. Ranking treats "demand" as one signal, but QBR-as-social-job (owner proving value to their client) and QBR-as-functional-job (operator reducing repeat work) are different jobs with different executors, and only the buyer's job has been interviewed (~93% buyer, EV-AB13/EV-AX26). Cormier ran a $4.5M shop and never hired this job (EV-AX01) — a nonconsumer, which JTBD treats as data. Test: switch-interview 5–8 *operators*, not owners.

**T2 · WTP for core.** Hole: **large, bordering fatal**. Nature: **evidence-contradiction**. "Build it into my RMM" ≥4/5 (EV-AB08) and Derek self-building "in a day" (EV-AX07) both say the job is small enough to solve with tools already hired. A job that size does not support a new hire, let alone a seven-layer stack (H76). Test: "what would you stop paying for to pay for this."

**T4 · Headless.** Hole: **moderate-large**. Nature: **framing-error**. Visibility of progress is *part of the job* (emotional job: control of delegated work), not overhead to design away — bookmarkable-app demand (EV-AX16), Stefanie resisting software dictating process (EV-AX17). Test: diary study of what MSPs check daily and why.

**T5 · OS framing.** Hole: **large**. Nature: **framing-error**. Stefanie proposed three surfaces (EV-AX18) — three jobs, not one. Bundling into "operating system" solves a job that doesn't exist at that scale.

**T6 · Earned autonomy.** Hole: **moderate**. Nature: **knowledge-gap**. SDT's home turf — noted, not owned.

**T7 · Does it work.** Hole: **fatal, unconditionally**. Nature: **evidence-contradiction**. No job gets hired for a product that doesn't do the job. 0/40 (EV-AB03) ends the conversation.

**T8 · Time-to-value.** Hole: **moderate**. Nature: **knowledge-gap**. Switch-cost math can't run — 15/20-min targets are unmeasured baselines (S35).

**T9 · Provenance.** Hole: **large**. Nature: **feasibility-wall on the ranking itself** — can't run switch-interviews against a ranking that mislabels its sample (U-EV01) and double-cites one WTP figure (U-EV02).

T3 skipped — plumbing is precondition-to-execute, not a claim about whether the job exists.

**Riskiest:** T1+T2 compound — job may not be distinctly hire-worthy *and* even where WTP exists it's small enough to solve with a tool already owned. A job too thin to justify a new relationship.
**Missing bet:** no theme is a **switch/fire study** — what MSPs do instead and why they'd abandon it. T1 measures ranked preference; JTBD measures displacement.
**Survival read:** ~25%. Kill: operator interviews confirm the QBR job is already satisfied by existing PSA/RMM bundling with no unmet residue.

### Service Blueprint (Shostack/Bitner) — the backstage isn't merely unbuilt; in two places it may be unbuildable

**T3 · Data access.** Hole: **large → fatal-if-true**. Nature: **feasibility-wall (legal/DPA egress ~40%, EV-AB09/EV-AX10) + evidence-contradiction (ledger $0, EV-AB14; 350 servers→9, EV-AX12)**. A legal wall isn't "not built yet," it's a chokepoint maybe unbuildable under current DPA terms. Test: one real messy mixed-vendor stack, full QBR pull end-to-end, measure completion rate.

**T4 · Headless.** Hole: **large**. Nature: **framing-error at blueprint level**. Headless removes the line of visibility — no frontstage surface to notice or intervene when the backstage AI is wrong. A blueprint with no line of interaction has no service-recovery path by construction.

**T6 · Earned autonomy.** Hole: **moderate-large**. Nature: **structural backstage gap**. The rogue-agent escalation path 13/17 fear (EV-AX14) is undesigned. Absence isn't "hasn't shipped," it's a hole in the model.

**T7 · Does it work.** Hole: **fatal** — this theme *is* "is the backstage built," answer 0/40 (EV-AB03), governance audit-after not control (EV-AB12).

**T5 · OS framing.** Hole: **large**. Nature: **framing-error**. One frontstage vs three (EV-AX18) is a literal blueprint decision; internal score ~5% vs ~45–50% (EV-AB15) says the wider blueprint is already suspect internally.

**T8 · Time-to-value.** Hole: **large**. Nature: **structural backstage gap disguised as a frontstage number**. PSA-connect is gated on open blocking questions (S43/S54/S55); the 20-min promise rests on a step not yet resolved as buildable.

T1, T2, T9 skipped — inputs to blueprint design.

**Riskiest:** T3 and T7 — both fail this lens's stage-appropriate test explicitly (legal wall = feasibility not developmental; "it works" = present-tense claim vs present-tense audit).
**Missing bet:** no **failure/exception-path design bet** — what happens frontstage+backstage when the AI is wrong and the client relationship is on the line. T6 covers granting trust, not recovering from a broken promise.
**Survival read:** ~15%. Kill: the DPA/legal wall proves non-negotiable across the major RMM/PSA vendors.

### Self-Determination Theory (Deci/Ryan) — the trust model's central claim is untested, and the clock to test it may be too short

**T4 · Headless.** Hole: **large**. Nature: **framing-error**. Invisibility removes the locus of control; bookmarkable-app demand (EV-AX16) + Stefanie (EV-AX17) read as autonomy-protective behavior, not UI preference.

**T6 · Earned autonomy.** Hole: **large — home ground**. Nature: **knowledge-gap → feasibility-wall**. Gated demand validated (EV-AX13/EV-AB11), but 13/17 fear rogue agents (EV-AX14) and "self-evident without explanation" (A33/A34) is never instrumented. Competence needs an *accurate* self-efficacy judgment; if admins can't assess what they grant, "earned autonomy" is compliance theater. Test: after onboarding, admins explain each trust level in their own words, score vs ground truth.

**T8 · Time-to-value.** Hole: **moderate-large**. Nature: **feasibility-wall on T6**. Competence builds through iteration; 15/20 min (S03/S41) may be too little clock for a real trust-delegation decision. T8 is partly a *cause* of T6's gap.

**T5 · OS framing.** Hole: **moderate**. Nature: **framing-error touching relatedness**. "Business-in-a-box" risks franchisee-identity vs independent-expert-identity; Dunlop "moat is runbooks not platform" (EV-AB05) fits practitioners identifying with their own competence.

**T1 · Real demand.** Hole: **moderate**. Nature: **knowledge-gap**. Operator's day-to-day sense of agency is unstudied.

**T7 · Does it work.** Hole: **moderate**. Nature: **evidence-contradiction, secondary**. A tool that fails in front of a client undermines operator competence/credibility — a reputational cost distinct from functional failure.

T2, T3, T9 skipped.

**Riskiest:** T6 (untested comprehension) and T4 (structurally autonomy-undermining).
**Missing bet:** no theme separates **operator-level agency from buyer-level demand** — whose autonomy/competence/relatedness the design serves; the daily technician ≠ the buying owner.
**Survival read:** ~30%. Kill: comprehension testing shows admins can't form accurate trust judgments inside the T8 window — collapses into dangerous over-trust or adoption-killing under-trust.

### Disruptive Innovation (Christensen) — the pricing points disruptive; the org is building sustaining against an incumbent who already owns the seat

**T5 · OS framing.** Hole: **large**. Nature: **framing-error**. ScalePad already runs OS framing (EV-AB16) — ACP-as-OS is sustaining feature-matching, not disruptive. Internal ~5% (EV-AB15) reads as recognition of a weak sustaining bet.

**T2 · WTP for core.** Hole: **large**. Nature: **evidence-contradiction + framing-error**. Disruptive signature is cheaper/simpler; WTP anchors (EV-AX05/EV-AB07) point at exactly that. The seven-layer premium stack (H76/H75/H83) is the opposite — fighting the wrong battle in Christensen's terms.

**T4 · Headless.** Hole: **moderate**, flagged for contradiction. Nature: **framing-error, collides with T5**. Headless *is* a legit disruptive strategy (attack from below, become plumbing) — but a different one than T5's OS-as-new-center. The crosswalk holds two disruption theories fighting each other, unchosen.

**T1 · Real demand.** Hole: **moderate**. Nature: **framing-error**. Cormier (EV-AX01) is a nonconsumer — the population disruptive plays target — not an absence of demand.

**T7 · Does it work.** Hole: **moderate**. Nature: **framing-error**. Disruptive products are allowed to be rough *if honestly framed as early*; claiming sustaining-grade maturity ("the technology works. It does.") for 0/40 (EV-AB03) invites incumbent-level scrutiny it can't survive.

**T9 · Provenance.** Hole: **moderate**. Nature: **feasibility-wall on market-sizing**. Any ceiling on the ~19–20-count buyer-skewed sample (U-EV01/U-EV05) isn't a market size.

T3, T6, T8 skipped.

**Riskiest:** T2+T5 — WTP says disruptive/cheap, built strategy says sustaining/premium OS, and ScalePad already occupies that seat.
**Missing bet:** **disruption-target identification** — nonconsumer (Cormier-shaped) vs existing-customer-of-incumbent (ScalePad-shaped); the nine themes conflate the two populations.
**Survival read:** ~20%. Kill: keep building premium/OS against ScalePad's occupied position while WTP points the other way — dies from terrain, not technology.

### Value Chain (Porter) — the chokepoint this thesis needs is legally blocked and competitively occupied at once

**T3 · Data access.** Hole: **fatal**. Nature: **feasibility-wall + chokepoint-ownership problem**. The system of record sits inside the PSA/RMM stack the *vendor* controls, not ACP. Legal egress walls (EV-AB09) + normalization 30%/75% (EV-AX11) = ACP is a supplicant to a chokepoint it doesn't own. No margin survives without control of its chokepoint.

**T2 · WTP for core.** Hole: **large**. Nature: **evidence-contradiction, coupled to T3**. Take-rate margin (H75–77) presumes ownership of the metered value; if T3's chokepoint isn't controlled and customers resist premium (EV-AX05), the ~$4B ceiling has no floor. One risk counted twice across themes.

**T5 · OS framing.** Hole: **large**. Nature: **positional chokepoint-occupancy problem** (distinct from T3's *access* problem). OS = chokepoint-maximizing rent (H76), but ScalePad sits there (EV-AB16) and no displacement mechanism is evidenced.

**T4 · Headless.** Hole: **moderate**. Nature: **framing-error, strategy-vs-execution mismatch**. Invisible infrastructure is a legit chokepoint-capture ("Intel Inside") move *if achievable* — but the buyer rejects it (EV-AX16). Strategy right / wrapper wrong, or strategy unavailable — crosswalk doesn't distinguish.

**T7 · Does it work.** Hole: **moderate-large**. Nature: **evidence-contradiction feeding T2/T3**. Can't extract rent for value you can't measure — ledger $0 (EV-AB14).

**T9 · Provenance.** Hole: **moderate**. Nature: **feasibility-wall on investment logic**. Where-to-insert decisions rest on unreliable position data (U-EV01/EV-AB17).

**T1 · Real demand.** Hole: **small — lens divergence, not a gap**. Buyer-only sample is arguably the *right* population for a margin/pricing question — unlike JTBD. Named as a place this lens and JTBD disagree.

T6, T8 skipped.

**Riskiest:** T3 and T5 — no margin claim survives without chokepoint access (legally blocked), and the position aimed for is already occupied with no displacement path.
**Missing bet:** **who structurally owns the chokepoint** — does ACP sit inside N-able's *own* value chain (owned-channel/home-field advantage) or is it trying to win data access from competitor-controlled PSA/RMM systems N-able doesn't own? Distinct from "can we access the data" (T3). `[CS: UNKNOWN — Beth's domain to characterize]`
**Survival read:** ~15%. Kill: T3 stays legally blocked *and* T5's chokepoint stays ScalePad-occupied with no displacement mechanism.

## Contradictions surfaced across voices (not reconciled)

> **T4 headless — strategy vs defect.** Disruptive Innovation + Value Chain call it a *legitimate* strategic move (attack-from-below / chokepoint-capture); Service Blueprint + SDT call the same bet a structural design defect (no line of visibility, autonomy-undermining). Evidence (EV-AX16/17) supports customer-rejection; whether that kills the *strategy* or only the *packaging* is unresolved.

> **T1 Cormier — nonconsumer vs no-job.** Disruptive reads "never ran a QBR" as an underserved disruptive segment (evidence *for*). JTBD reads it as evidence the job may not exist worth hiring for. Same fact, opposite implication.

> **T1 buyer-sample — right vs wrong population.** Value Chain calls the ~93% buyer sample arguably *correct* for a margin/pricing question; JTBD calls it the central defect since the operator is the job's executor. A decision about which question the themes answer, not an evidence gap.

> **T7 does-it-work — kill vs reframe.** Service Blueprint treats 0/40 as fatal on its own terms; Disruptive treats it as forgivable *if honestly framed early* — the concern is the claim-vs-gap mismatch, not the gap. Same evidence, one says kill, one says reframe.
