## Overview
Pulseway is a cloud-based RMM platform differentiated by its mobile-first design — the entire platform is accessible and operable from a smartphone app. It serves MSPs and IT teams who want to monitor and respond to IT issues from anywhere without being tethered to a laptop. Pulseway includes monitoring, automation, patch management, remote control, and backup capabilities in a single platform.

## MSP Relevance
Pulseway is relevant to smaller MSPs and solo technicians who manage client environments with a mobile-centric workflow. Its real-time notifications and mobile response capabilities make it popular with MSPs who are on-site frequently and need to react quickly. It is not typically a primary RMM for large MSPs but serves a loyal niche among mobile-first operators.

## Key Offerings & Capabilities
- Mobile-first RMM — full monitoring, alerting, and response via smartphone app (iOS and Android)
- Real-time monitoring and alerts — instant push notifications with one-tap response
- Patch management — Windows, macOS, and third-party application patching
- Remote control — full remote desktop from mobile device or web browser
- Automated scripting and remediation
- Pulseway Backup — integrated endpoint backup
- PSA integration — ticketing and basic service management

## Recent Announcements
- Continued platform development focused on mobile experience improvements
- Automation and scripting library expansion
- Integration updates with major PSA and security tools
- Pulseway maintains its niche positioning as the mobile-first RMM for technicians on the go

## Pricing Model
Per-device per-month. Pulseway Team and Business tiers. Pricing starts around $27/month for up to 5 systems; scales per device. Free tier available for personal use (up to 2 systems). MSP pricing available with volume discounts.

## Strengths
- Best mobile RMM experience in the market — genuinely full-featured smartphone app
- Real-time push notifications with immediate one-tap response actions
- Affordable entry pricing for small MSPs
- Simple setup with low technical overhead

## Weaknesses / Gaps
- Less feature depth than NinjaOne, ConnectWise, or Kaseya for larger MSP operations
- Automation and scripting less mature than RMM-focused competitors
- Limited AI or intelligent automation features
- Not competitive for MSPs managing 500+ devices across multiple clients

## Competitive Threat to NZO
Level: Low
Reason: Pulseway serves a niche mobile-first segment and lacks the platform depth to compete with NZO's AI agent and automation capabilities. The overlap is minimal.

## Notes
- Mobile-first design is a genuine UX differentiator that larger RMM vendors have not matched
- Pulseway MSPs who grow often migrate to NinjaOne, ConnectWise, or Kaseya — potential NZO customer journey
- Worth monitoring for any AI automation announcements that could shift its positioning
