What happened

An attacker phished credentials from m.alvarez (Finance Ops), bypassed MFA via push fatigue, then ran encoded PowerShell on FIN-WS-04 to dump LSASS and pivot via SMB to the finance database server FIN-DB-01, where they installed a WinRM listener and established a C2 beacon to 185.220.x.x.

What was done

Alpha auto-isolated FIN-WS-04 and FIN-DB-01 from the network at 14:29:14. The m.alvarez session and all active tokens were revoked. The newly enrolled device WIN-7K2P was quarantined. C2 destination added to the deny list. Incident handoff packet sent to on-call analyst.

What to do next

Force credential reset for m.alvarez and svc_backup. Image FIN-WS-04 for forensics — confirm initial phishing vector. Review last 7 days of finance database access for svc_backup. Brief Finance leadership before market open. Pilot phishing-resistant MFA for Finance group.
