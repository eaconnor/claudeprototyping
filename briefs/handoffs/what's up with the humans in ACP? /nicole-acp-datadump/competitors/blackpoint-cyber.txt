## Overview
Blackpoint Cyber is an MDR platform built exclusively for the MSP channel, offering 24/7 SOC-backed threat detection and response. In 2025, Blackpoint launched CompassOne — a unified security posture and response platform that extends beyond MDR into vulnerability management, asset inventory, and posture rating. The company's SOC disrupted 56% of incidents before attackers could deploy a payload in 2025, a core differentiator in their threat report messaging.

## MSP Relevance
Blackpoint is one of the highest-trust MDR vendors in the MSP community, known for speed of response and MSP-first delivery. CompassOne's posture management and asset visibility give MSPs a security maturity story to tell clients, not just incident response. The NinjaOne partnership (August 2025) creates a compelling bundled RMM + MDR narrative for MSPs.

## Key Offerings & Capabilities
- Managed Detection and Response (MDR) — 24/7 SOC with rapid containment
- CompassOne platform (launched April 2025) — unified security posture and response
- Security Posture Rating — quantifies MSP and customer security maturity
- Unified Asset Inventory — attack surface visibility across endpoints
- Vulnerability Management — integrated within CompassOne
- Tenant Administrator — MSP oversight layer for multi-client management
- NinjaOne integration (August 2025) — combined RMM + MDR in a single partner stack

## Recent Announcements
- Launched CompassOne at RSA Conference April 2025 — unified posture management platform
- NinjaOne partnership announced August 2025 — integrated MDR with RMM management
- Published 2026 Annual Threat Report (April 2026) — major finding: attackers shifting to trusted credentials and tools over exploits; SOC disrupted 56% of incidents pre-payload in 2025
- Right of Boom 2026 event recap highlights credential abuse as the dominant attack vector

## Pricing Model
Per-endpoint per-month through MSP partner tier. MDR pricing is consumption-based on managed devices. CompassOne adds posture management at an additional per-endpoint fee. Exact pricing requires partner agreement.

## Strengths
- Exclusively MSP-focused — no direct enterprise sales competing with partners
- SOC response speed — known for stopping attacks before payload deployment
- CompassOne gives MSPs a proactive security story, not just reactive MDR
- NinjaOne partnership creates distribution leverage across NinjaOne's large MSP base
- Threat intelligence from 2026 report is actionable and well-communicated to MSPs

## Weaknesses / Gaps
- Smaller brand footprint than CrowdStrike, SentinelOne, or even Huntress
- CompassOne is new and still maturing in feature depth
- Limited outside of North America
- Less AI automation in response workflows compared to CrowdStrike or SentinelOne

## Competitive Threat to NZO
Level: Low
Reason: Blackpoint is focused on security MDR and posture, not workflow automation or AI agent management. The NinjaOne partnership creates a bundled narrative that could reduce openings for additional tools, but the core overlap with NZO is minimal.

## Notes
- NinjaOne partnership is strategically important — if NinjaOne + Blackpoint becomes a default MSP bundle, it reduces stack openings for NZO
- CompassOne posture rating gives MSPs a sellable security narrative — watch for similar features in competing platforms
- 2026 threat report finding (credential abuse over exploits) is important context for MSP security strategy
