## AI Automation Capabilities

N-able identifies several key automation functions in security operations:

### Threat Triage & Investigation
"Automated workflows act as the first line of defense" by ingesting alerts and cross-referencing threat intelligence to close false positives and assess severity.

### Immediate Containment
Systems can autonomously isolate infected endpoints, terminate malicious processes, and revoke compromised credentials to prevent lateral movement.

### Recovery Workflows
Automation streamlines post-attack recovery through automated backup verification and data restoration processes.

### Routine Tasks Recommended for Automation
- User onboarding/offboarding
- Patch deployment
- Phishing email quarantine
- Endpoint isolation based on behavioral indicators

## Human-in-the-Loop Approach

N-able advocates a hybrid model: [CS: VERIFIED]

**"You configure the system for approval on high-stakes actions. The AI does the prep work, collecting data and queuing actions, but a human makes the final call."**

Article emphasizes that automation "amplifies" professional expertise rather than replacing it, positioning humans as decision-makers for critical actions while AI handles preparation and lower-risk tasks.
