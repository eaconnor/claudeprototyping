# Visual/material vocabulary — named techniques, 1980s girl-branding aesthetic

## Airbrush gradient illustration
Dominant commercial-art technique of the decade. Smooth, seamless color gradients; slight manual inconsistencies distinguish traditional airbrush from later digital gradients. Associated with commercial illustrators of the era (e.g. Patrick Nagel — note: Nagel's own published work, e.g. in Playboy, is adult/fashion-oriented, not girl-toy branding; cited here for technique lineage only, not as a direct visual reference for this project).

## Holographic/iridescent foil and neon ink
Specifically documented as a Lisa Frank technical innovation applied to stickers and packaging — neon ink combined with metallic/holographic sticker effects.
