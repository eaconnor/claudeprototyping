  • 47 → 12 is the screenshot. Carla's value prop becomes a number you can put on a slide. Sales will use this.

⬤ What will hurt you
  • You're staking an AI feature on guesses. Probabilistic scoring is stochastic — it behaves differently every time. Without Julian validating with 10–12 analysts, you're shipping something nobody verified actually works under real load. NN/g calls 5 the floor for normal features; AI features need 10–12. Nobody's done that yet.
  • Hilda is one customer. She might be right about everything. She might be the only one. If you build for Hilda and the MSP segment hates it, that's a feature-shaped product nobody buys. Julian needs 10–16 more customer voices before this proposition is a market claim.
  • You haven't drawn the current state. "Analysts hunt across alerts" is a sentence, not a map. Alaina has to Service Blueprint the current MDR workflow before she can design the replacement — otherwise you'll redesign one screen and break three handoffs.
  • This prototype isn't Apex. The visual looks right but the components are HTML mockups, the tokens are approximated, and the data is fake. Alaina + Bryce Wilson need to bind this to Apex Assembly via schema.json or production will look like a different product.
  • Alert fatigue is emotional. You wrote it as operational. Analysts feel exposed when they miss a real one. The UI tone — the words, the urgency, the suppression language — depends on the Feel data Julian hasn't gathered. Right now you're guessing.
  • This brief is an orphan. "Expanded Multi-Event Correlation" is referenced but not linked. When portfolio review asks "what bigger initiative does this ladder up to?" you'll be improvising. 15 minutes to fix.
  • You can't prove this wrong. Success criteria say what good looks like; nothing says what would make Alpha a bad bet. If the MDR beta lands at "kind of works" — what then? Add kill criteria before beta, not after.
