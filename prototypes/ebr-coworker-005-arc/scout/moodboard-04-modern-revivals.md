# Modern intentional revivals of the 1980s unicorn/rainbow-glam aesthetic

## Starbucks Unicorn Frappuccino (April 2017)
Limited-edition, color-changing drink; explicitly documented as the center of a viral "unicorn trend." Visual grammar: pastel gradients, high saturation pink/violet/baby-blue, glitter, holographic surfaces, iridescent finishes, sprinkles. Framed in coverage as nostalgia-driven "regression-escapism" tied to 80s/90s childhood.
[CS: HIGH — well-documented, multiple corroborating sources]
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unicorn_Frappuccino

## Too Faced "Unicorn Tears" / Unicorn Collection (highlighters, cosmetics, ~2018)
Unicorn-horn-shaped highlighter sticks (twisted/knotted rope form), opalescent/color-shifting pearl finish (blue-to-violet shift). Companion "Unicorn Dreams" shade (golden rose). Broader collection included a 13-pan eyeshadow palette infused with crystals and color-changing lipsticks. Direct beauty-industry example of the aesthetic modernized into a premium product rather than literal childhood pastiche.
[CS: HIGH — multiple independent retail/review sources corroborate]

## Barbie Dreamtopia unicorn line (Mattel, ongoing)
Direct continuation/revival of the Barbie-unicorn pairing: pink/yellow/purple pastel manes, glitter headbands, "Sweetville Kingdom" fantasy branding, color-change play features.
[CS: MEDIUM] — product-line existence and current retail presentation confirmed; no design-rationale or brand-strategy source found explaining it as a deliberate "80s revival," only retail listings.
