## Cross-framework convergence — what all three agree on

1. **Enter > Engage.** 5Es, AEIOU-Interactions, and council JRNY-2 all point at the same thing: the connect-your-stack moment is the untested make-or-break, and we're testing the stage after it.
2. **The document is two documents.** 5Es-Exit, AEIOU-Interactions/Objects, Human-Factors-Social, and council JRNY-3 all surface the frontstage (client) / backstage (MSP) split. Provenance + gaps live backstage; the client export must be seamless. One entity in the brief, two in reality.
3. **Cultural/competence is the sleeper risk.** The only human factor with zero data is the one (SDT competence) that may decide whether MSPs adopt or feel replaced. Every other framework assumes they *want* the work gone; SDT says they may want to still feel like the author. This is the same tension as the ownership-attribution "X% you" idea — the coworker must leave the MSP feeling like the author.
4. **Extend is a grounded opportunity we haven't productized.** Portal + CRM pipeline + quarter-over-quarter follow-through are real MSP practice [D] and absent from the brief.

## D. Doblin Ten Types — anything to play with

Building on council PORT-1's two-column score (brief-as-written vs brief + N-able latent capability). The brief plays **two** types (Product Performance, Process); N-able *holds* six more it never surfaces. Each latent type is a concrete play:

| Type | The play | Force | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Product System** ⭐ | Position the QBR as "the review that writes itself because the data already lives in your N-able stack (N-central + Cove + N-sight)." The coworker isn't standalone — it's the *system* surfacing. | Strong | The strategy the brief left on the table. Turns the feasibility headwind (6–8 integrations) into a head-start for N-able's own stack. |
| **Network** ⭐ | **Data-network moat.** Every MSP that connects its stack improves the cross-vendor normalization mapping for all. The "nobody normalizes cross-stack" gap (today [WOBBLY] absence-of-evidence) becomes a *compounding* moat a VC rollup can't copy by acquiring MSPs. | Strong | The one play that answers "Derek could build it in a day / Vikram already did." A solo build has no network. This is the defensible-differentiation answer the council kept circling. |
| **Service** | N-able's MSP-success org backstage-supports the "this number looks wrong" moment (the support gap council JRNY-3 flagged). "When a number's off, you have someone to call" — the trust safety-net made operational. | Medium | Incumbency is a *tailwind* here, not a headwind. |
| **Channel** | Launch through the existing N-able sales motion + partner community, not as a net-new standalone acquisition. | Medium | Underused; de-risks the Adjacent bet (PORT-3). |
| **Brand** | The two-sided document IS a brand play: **"powered by N-able" backstage, MSP-branded frontstage.** The MSP's client never sees N-able; the MSP does. Resolves the double-edged brand finding (recognition helps adoption, "RMM vendor" priming caps WTP). | Medium | Frontstage/backstage split is a brand architecture, not just an IA one. |
| **Profit Model** ⚠️ | The headwind. Play = do NOT price standalone (own RMM caps WTP). Price as a **retention-defense add-on** or usage-tiered by customer-count so it doesn't cannibalize the RMM subscription. | Must-decide | This is the T4 fork (sustaining vs standalone). A pricing *decision*, not a design move — routes to Beth, Meaghan (her boss), and Nicole. |

**Two plays worth prototyping into the experience, not just the strategy:**
- **Network (⭐):** show, in the Enter/onboarding surface, that connecting your stack makes the normalization smarter for everyone — a visible network-effect, not a hidden one. This is the moat made *felt*.
- **Product System (⭐):** in Step 2, visually distinguish "data from your N-able stack (instant, clean)" from "data reconciled across third-party tools (the hard part)." Honest about the Process advantage being *partial* (council PORT-1) AND it surfaces the system. Doubles as the honest normalization-pretense declaration the critic demanded (critic-pass-1 gap #2).

**Headwind to keep named:** Structure (internal ownership: does the QBR coworker team control the N-central roadmap? [ASSUMPTION]) and Profit Model (RMM cannibalization). Being the incumbent is a tailwind on Service/Channel/Network/Brand and a headwind on Profit Model/Structure. The brief surfaces neither.
