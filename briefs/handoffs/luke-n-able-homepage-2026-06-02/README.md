## What's testing in this packet

**Hypothesis:** Gray-led palette (vs. saturated brand colors) increases perceived enterprise credibility and CTA prominence for MSP decision-makers.

**Not testing:**
- Whether the homepage needs to change
- What the current conversion rate is
- Whether gray differentiates n-able from competitors
- Whether MSPs respond positively to neutral palettes
- Whether n-able's brand guidelines permit gray

All of the above are **[?] Unknown** in the brief. This is a design direction from Beth, not a validated problem with customer evidence.

## Council found 7 tensions

These are **not flaws** — they're design decisions Luke must make. Council voices (Kano, JTBD, Service Blueprint, Wardley, Disruptive Innovation, Blue Ocean, Self-Determination Theory) offer conflicting interpretations. That's the point.

1. **Gray as performance quality vs. brand liability** (Kano vs. Brand Equity Theory)
2. **No job-to-be-done for gray** (JTBD framework)
3. **Frontstage signal vs. backstage scaffold** (Service Blueprint vs. Wardley Map)
4. **Disruption direction unknown** (sustaining upmarket? low-end? new market?)
5. **Differentiation without competitive canvas** (Blue Ocean vs. Competitive Analysis)
6. **No margin story** (Porter's Value Chain)
7. **Competence and autonomy under neutral palette** (Self-Determination Theory)

Read `council-fast.md` for full testimony. Tensions are not resolved — they're routed to Luke.
