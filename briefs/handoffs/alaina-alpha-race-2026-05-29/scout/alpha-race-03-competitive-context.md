## Positioning tension (Geoffrey Moore)

**Moore positioning statement (implied):**  
"For MDR analysts overwhelmed by alert volume, Alpha RACE consolidates 47 events into 12 incidents with transparent scoring. Unlike Huntress/Arctic Wolf, Alpha shows *why* each incident scored the way it did." [CS: MEDIUM — reconstructed from prototype value prop + competitive claim]

**Blindspot (council voice LAND-05):**  
Moore positioning assumes the competitor is another vendor. Sometimes the real competitor is the analyst's own workaround: ignore lists, filter rules, learned numbness. If the competitor is "I just ignore most alerts," then Alpha is competing against **resignation**, not Huntress. [CS: HIGH — council voice insight]

### Implication
Demo needs to address both:
1. "Alpha is better than Huntress" (vendor displacement)
2. "Alpha is better than your current coping mechanism" (behavior change)

The prototype focuses on (1). It does not address (2). [CS: MEDIUM — inferred from prototype content review]

## KANO vs. Self-Determination Theory tension

**KANO Model** says transparency is an **Attractive** feature (delighter — customers don't expect it, but love it when they see it). [CS: MEDIUM — council voice NEEDS-06]

**Self-Determination Theory (SDT)** says transparency may threaten **Competence** (one of three psychological needs). If the analyst sees "Why 94" and doesn't understand it, they may feel deskilled. [CS: MEDIUM — council voice NEEDS-03]

### Tension
Both frameworks are valid. Transparency nets positive *if* the analyst feels **more competent** ("now I understand the system"). Transparency nets negative *if* the analyst feels **less competent** ("the system knows more than me"). [CS: HIGH — council synthesis]

**Prototype makes a bet:** Transparency nets positive. **Evidence:** None. [CS: VERIFIED — council voice NEEDS-06 states this explicitly]

## Recommendation from competitive context

1. **Run competitive feature audit.** Validate whether Huntress/Arctic Wolf actually have multi-source correlation + transparency. Do not assume differentiation — verify it. [CS: MEDIUM — standard competitive analysis practice]

2. **Test transparency with analysts first.** Before showing "Why 94" to customers, show it to 3–5 MDR analysts and ask: "Does this make you feel more competent or less competent?" KANO vs. SDT tension is real. [CS: HIGH — council voice recommendation]

3. **Map the real competitor.** If the competitor is "analyst resignation" (ignoring alerts), the demo narrative must address behavior change, not just vendor displacement. [CS: HIGH — council voice LAND-05 insight]
