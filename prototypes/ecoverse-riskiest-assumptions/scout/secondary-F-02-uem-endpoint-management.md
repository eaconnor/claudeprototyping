## The structural observation, stated flat

- Highest CAGR in the pool: **Mordor, 25.38%** — the **only** publisher that states a sizing formula.
- Lowest CAGR in the pool: **Worldwide Market Reports, 10.6%** — the publisher with the **least** disclosure and the weakest provenance.
- The blended endpoint-security totals (M&M 9.6%, Fortune 8.60%) sit near the bottom, but they size a **different, larger market** that includes EPP/EDR.
- The private Gartner benchmark says "moderate."

This is not a resolution. It is the shape of the disagreement. [WOBBLY] on any claim that the pool converges.
