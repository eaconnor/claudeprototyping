# F-03 — Patch Management as Its Own Segment

**Beth's question:** does anyone size patch management on its own? **Yes — at least five publishers do.** Directly relevant to her patching / patching+vuln-remediation wedge candidates.

**Caution up front:** the base-year figures span ~USD 0.66B to USD 2.72B for adjacent years. That is a ~4x spread. The category boundary is not agreed.

## Where the consensus actually is

Excluding openPR and the MRFR outlier, four publishers land at **8.30% – 10.5% CAGR** on a base of roughly **USD 0.7B – 1.0B**:
- IMARC 8.30%
- Fact.MR 9%
- MRFR 10.38% (but on a 3x base)
- Grand View 10.4%
- Data Bridge 10.5% (arithmetic suspect)

That is the tightest agreement of any segment in this pass. [CS: MEDIUM — four of five sources are snippet-only]

**No blending or averaging performed.** No single publisher stated a consensus figure and none is constructed here.

## Scope limit — stated plainly

**No publisher scopes patch management to the endpoint.** None of these six distinguishes endpoint patching from server, OS, application, firmware or cloud-workload patching. If Ecoverse's wedge is specifically *endpoint* patching, that number **is not separately sized by anyone found in this pass.** [CS: HIGH — negative finding]

M&M, meanwhile, bundles patch with vulnerability into a single Type ("Patch & Vulnerability Management") and publishes no figure for it.
