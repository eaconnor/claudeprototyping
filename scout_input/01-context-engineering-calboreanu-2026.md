SOURCE_URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04258
AUTHOR: Elias Calboreanu
DATE: April 5, 2026
TYPE: arXiv preprint (not peer-reviewed)
CS_BASE: MEDIUM — observational, single operator, no controlled comparison group

## Abstract / Core Claim
"Context completeness may be more strongly associated with output quality" than prompting technique alone. The paper proposes a structured methodology for assembling informational context around prompts.

## Key Methodology
Five-role context package structure:
- Authority
- Exemplar
- Constraint
- Rubric
- Metadata

Four-phase pipeline: Reviewer → Design → Builder → Auditor

Applied reliability engineering and information theory as analytical frameworks.

## Empirical Findings
Across 200 documented interactions with four AI tools (Claude, ChatGPT, Cowork, Codex):
- Incomplete context correlated with 72% of iteration cycles
- Structured context reduced average iterations from 3.8 to 2.0 per task
- First-pass acceptance improved from 32% to 55%
- Final success rate: 91.5% when iteration was permitted (183 of 200 tasks)

LIMITATION: Results are observational from a single operator without controlled comparison groups.
