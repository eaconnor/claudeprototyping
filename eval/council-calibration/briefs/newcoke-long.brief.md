## Gate 3 — Built right

1. Single-sip taste preference in a blind test does not predict full-can or repeat/ritual consumption preference — a different consumption context than the test measured.
2. Underestimates emotional and identity-based attachment to the original as a product beyond its flavor profile, which a taste test cannot capture.
