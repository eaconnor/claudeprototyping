## Article 14 reporting clocks

Per the Commission cra-summary page:
- **24 hours** — "early warning notification"
- **72 hours** — "main" notification
- **14 days** — final report, after corrective measures become available (actively exploited vulnerability)
- **One month** — final report for severe incidents

## Support period / security updates

Commission cra-summary, on the manufacturer's duty:
> manufacturers must "determine a support period for its product with digital elements, during which the manufacturer will ensure that vulnerabilities of that product are handled effectively."

The end date must be "clearly and understandably specified at the time of purchase."

> **The CRA does not specify a fixed number of years for the support period — manufacturers set this duration themselves.** [CS: HIGH — this is the Commission summary page's own characterisation]

**[CS: FABRICATION RISK — named explicitly]** A "5-year default support period" is widely repeated in secondary commentary. It was **not** found on either Commission page read. Do not state it. Article 13(8) of the primary text is where to check.

## Scope

Commission: applies to "products with digital elements" made available on the EU market. Examples given: "baby-monitors to smart watches, from apps to computer programs, connectable hardware and software." Enforcement by national market surveillance authorities; CE marking indicates compliance. [CS: VERIFIED — Commission]

Commission's problem statement: the Act "addresses the inadequate level of cybersecurity in many products, and the lack of timely security updates." [CS: VERIFIED — Commission]
