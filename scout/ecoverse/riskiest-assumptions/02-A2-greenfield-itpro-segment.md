## Read
- **Contradicts** the assumption further than the deck's own internal contradiction already did. All three named competitors in this space (Action1, Flamingo, Intune) show 2026 momentum, not retreat: Action1 growing fastest-in-category and now moving into MSP channel, Flamingo raising more capital and widening scope, Intune getting cheaper and more included for the exact buyer profile. No independent 2026 evidence found supporting "greenfield" — nothing found describing IT Pro &lt;1000 endpoints as underserved or open in the current market.
- **Riskiness:** High. If wrong, Phase 1 sequencing logic (IT Pro first because it's "easier"/"more open") is built on a segment three well-funded/well-distributed competitors are simultaneously tightening their grip on, not one that's empty.
- **Test cost:** Low-to-moderate — this doesn't need new primary research, it needs the deck's own contradiction to be resolved as a decision (kill "greenfield" or defend it with a business-model angle, per critic P0 #2 and council's Disruptive Innovation lens) plus a scan (which this pass partially did) of competitor momentum before the kickoff room anchors on the word.
