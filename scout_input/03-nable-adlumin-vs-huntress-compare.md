# Source 03 — N-able's OWN "Adlumin vs Huntress" comparison page

URL: https://www.n-able.com/compare/adlumin-vs-huntress

Type: VENDOR-AUTHORED competitive comparison (N-able is Adlumin's owner). [WOBBLY] — heavily Adlumin-favorable; treat every "Huntress lacks X" as a claim to verify, NOT neutral fact. Useful mainly because it names the exact dimensions N-able chooses to compete on.

## The 10 comparison dimensions N-able selects (verbatim framing)
1. **Time to Protection** — Adlumin "cloud-based onboarding and streamlined workflows" vs Huntress "end-to-end readiness" (variable). [CS: LOW — vendor-biased]
2. **Coverage and Visibility** — Adlumin "correlates activity across endpoints, identities, logs, network, and cloud"; Huntress "limited native visibility into network traffic and broader cloud services." [CS: LOW — vendor-biased]
3. **Detection Model** — Adlumin "automation first MDR/XDR"; Huntress "human-led analysis." [CS: MEDIUM — both sides corroborate this framing elsewhere]
4. **Containment** — Adlumin "built-in automated response playbooks"; Huntress "operator-executed actions or guidance." [CS: MEDIUM]
5. **Platform Consolidation** — Adlumin "consolidates MDR, SIEM, and SOAR into one platform"; Huntress "fewer integrations into broader IT." [CS: LOW — vendor-biased]
6. **Investigation Transparency** — Adlumin "self-service access to underlying investigation data"; Huntress findings "primarily through managed workflows." [CS: LOW — vendor-biased]
7. **Audit Readiness** — Adlumin "audit ready case files"; Huntress "remediation summaries" w/o detailed artifacts. [CS: LOW — vendor-biased]
8. **Compliance/Posture** — Adlumin "built-in posture insights"; Huntress "support primarily through reporting." [CS: LOW — vendor-biased]
9. **Breach Warranty** — Only Adlumin includes it; Huntress does not. [CS: MEDIUM — corroborated by PeerSpot, source 04]
10. **Pricing Structure** — Adlumin "simple, per endpoint pricing"; Huntress "multiple SKUs." [CS: MEDIUM]

## Note
These are the factors N-able itself chose for the FEAT cluster — i.e., the dimensions Adlumin believes it wins on. The absence of SAT, Smart Filtering, and EDR maturity from this list is itself a signal (factors N-able does NOT raise). Do not read the omissions as Huntress weaknesses.
