Don't configure a coworker. Show it the report you already make. This is the first-run flow: you bring last quarter's EBR, it reads the structure, works out which sources feed which section, and drafts a coworker. It never activates itself — that's acp-core 004-ai-coworker-registry User Story 4, and the draft/activate split is a real table in that spec. What's fabricated here: the parse quality and every confidence number.

Show me your report and I'll build you a thing

Six steps, about four minutes. The last one is a human clicking activate — deliberately, because a coworker that switches itself on is a coworker nobody agreed to.

◐ TIER 1 · CONCEPT

Tests one bet: that an MSP's existing EBR contains enough structure to draft a coworker from, so onboarding is "show us what you do" rather than "fill in this config."

What's real

  • The draft→activate split, draft locks, and immutable versions are real tables in acp-core 004-ai-coworker-registry (42/46 done) — not invented for this mock.
  • Guardrails and Triggers are real JSONB shapes with Pydantic models upstream (GuardrailConfig, TriggerConfig discriminated union).
  • The connector availability per source is scouted and sourced, not assumed.

What's fabricated

  • The parse itself. Reading structure out of a real 8-tab spreadsheet is the hard part and none of it happens here.
  • Every confidence number on the inference table.

What will hurt you

  • Structure is inferable. Judgment isn't. The report says a finding was amber; it never says why. That gap doesn't close at onboarding — it closes over cycles, or never.
  • An MSP whose EBR is a slide deck with no consistent structure gets a much worse draft than one with an 8-tab workbook. This flow flatters the organised.
  • The EBR Review primitive is scaffolded, not confirmed. If it lands structurally different, every surface built against it needs rework, not relabeling.
  • The "8" is still unreconciled — at least four non-equivalent 8s live in the corpus (8 tabs, 8 connectors, 8 sources, 8 integrations). This artifact says "8 tabs" throughout and may be counting the wrong thing.
  • Nobody has decided what happens when the draft is wrong in a way the MSP doesn't notice until cycle three.
