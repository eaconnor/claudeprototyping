The proportions that actually read as "unicorn" at a glance: a short, gently-curved jaw with a visible nostril notch, one round eye placed high on the face, a straight-sided triangular horn (not spiraled — that reads as decorative rather than as the recognizable feature), and a mane that flows in a smooth S-curve down the back of the neck.
