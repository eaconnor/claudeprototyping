## Things that we know (per document)

1. Partners struggle to manage Cloud services with Microsoft native tools
2. Partners would like to manage and monitor Cloud from a single pane of glass
3. Currently there is no product on the market that sufficiently satisfies the needs of MSPs/CSPs
4. Security and compliance is top of mind for all CSPs
5. Partners seek to scale their businesses by increasing their efficiency and optimizing cost
