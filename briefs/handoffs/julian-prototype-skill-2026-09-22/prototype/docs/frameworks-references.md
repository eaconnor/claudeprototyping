# Frameworks — references and links

Every method in the library, with its canonical citation. Grouped by tradition code (matching `methods-library-matrix.md`). Numbers are the library's method IDs.

Confidence discipline applies to a bibliography too: citations below are well-established published works [CS: HIGH] unless tagged otherwise. Before quoting a specific page or formulation in your own work, check the primary source — that's the point of this file.

## IIT — IIT Institute of Design (the spine)

The lineage: Bauhaus (Moholy-Nagy, New Bauhaus Chicago, 1937) → IIT Institute of Design → Doblin / Kumar / Owen / Keeley.

**Charles L. Owen — Structured Planning** *(03 Design Factor · 07 Project Statement · 08 Defining Statements · 30 RELATN · 38 Quality Pyramid · 40 Means/Ends · 41 Metaplanning · 42 two-step development · 43 Originator/Contributors)*
- Owen, C.L., "Structured Planning in Design: Information-Age Tools for Product Development," *Design Issues* 17(1), Winter 2001, pp. 27–43.
- IIT Institute of Design publications archive: https://id.iit.edu
- See `owen-structured-planning.md` in this repo for what each instrument does and how the matrix math descends from RELATN. [CS: MEDIUM on "Quality Pyramid" as a canonical Owen label — verify against primary sources; it may be this library's shorthand for Owen's concept-evaluation structure.]

**Vijay Kumar — the seven modes** *(04 Mode 1 · 33 Mode 2 · 23 Mode 3 · 05 Mode 4 · 36 Mode 5 · 37 Mode 6 · 39 Mode 7)*
- Kumar, V., *101 Design Methods: A Structured Approach for Driving Innovation in Your Organization*, Wiley, 2013.
- Mode mapping used in this library: 1 Sense Intent (semiotics) · 2 Know Context (landscape) · 3 Know People (ethnography) · 4 Frame Insights (hypothesis) · 5 Explore Concepts (concept gen) · 6 Frame Solutions (evaluation) · 7 Realize Offerings (realize).

**Doblin — Ten Types of Innovation** *(12)*
- Keeley, L., Walters, H., Pikkel, R., Quinn, B., *Ten Types of Innovation: The Discipline of Building Breakthroughs*, Wiley, 2013. https://www.deloitte.com (Doblin is a Deloitte business)
