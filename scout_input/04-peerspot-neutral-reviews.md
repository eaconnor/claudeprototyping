# Source 04 — PeerSpot user-review comparison (neutral-ish)

URL: https://www.peerspot.com/products/comparisons/adlumin-security-operations_vs_huntress-managed-siem

Type: Aggregated end-user reviews. Closest thing to neutral in the pool, BUT:
[WOBBLY] — sample is lopsided and tiny: Huntress n=7 reviews, Adlumin n=1 review. Ratings below are NOT statistically meaningful. Do not treat sentiment scores as signal.

## Reported ratings (low-N, use with caution)
- Adlumin: 0% willing to recommend, 1 review, 3.3 sentiment. [CS: LOW — n=1]
- Huntress: 100% willing to recommend, 7 reviews, 8.8 rating, 6.1 sentiment. [CS: MEDIUM — n=7]
- Mindshare: Huntress #26 in SIEM (1.1%); Adlumin #52 (0.9%). [CS: MEDIUM]

## Features praised
Huntress: "single pane of glass management with other security products"; real-time detection with human analyst review; strong compliance support; rapid incident response. [CS: MEDIUM]
Adlumin: better reporting; easier implementation; cost-effective pricing. [CS: LOW — n=1]

## Pricing comments
Huntress: "cost-efficient," charged per data source not data size, "very well-priced for its capabilities." [CS: MEDIUM]
Adlumin: "cost-effective pricing with satisfactory ROI," but "room for improvement in SIEM pricing structures." [CS: LOW — n=1]

## Cons / criticisms (carry into Kano "must-have gaps")
Huntress: limited third-party integrations (esp. non-Microsoft); scalability challenges w/ high log volumes & multi-tenant; lacks some EDR integrations like SentinelOne. [CS: MEDIUM]
Adlumin: "Implementation and integration with Office 365 was not as straightforward"; fewer total integrations vs alternatives. [CS: LOW — n=1]
