The Problem

Why Traditional Detection Fails

Most detection tools were built for a threat landscape that no longer exists. LOTL attacks operate in the gaps.

No Malware to Catch

84% of severe attacks use only legitimate tools. Signatures look for known bad. LOTL attacks use known good — PowerShell, WMI, scheduled tasks, PsExec. There is no payload to detect. The attack is the administration.

Humans Can't Scale

A single analyst reviewing PowerShell logs at scale would need to check 1 billion commands a week. That's not a staffing problem. It's a math problem. Human review, even with a full SOC, cannot keep pace with LOTL volume.

Alerts Without Context Erode Trust

Binary detection rules produce noise. Analysts learn to ignore alerts. When everything is flagged, nothing is urgent. The real threat slides through the same channel as ten thousand false positives — and no one flinches.
