### Self-Determination Theory -- The design undermines autonomy (user cannot use own produce or override the pack lock) and competence (device is inert without a live connection and a valid subscription), and the strongest objection is the trust model requires surrendering agency to a proprietary supply chain the brief never justifies.

### Disruptive Innovation -- This reads as sustaining/premium innovation aimed at existing juice-conscious affluent consumers rather than a disruptive low-end or new-market play, and the strongest objection is it has no moat to defend and no cheap foothold to attack from, so it's exposed on both flanks.

### Value Chain -- The capability inserts at the "preparation" node of the customer's health/wellness value chain with margin designed to live in recurring proprietary pack sales (razor/blade economics), and the strongest objection is the chokepoint (the pack) is only a chokepoint if the press mechanism can't be bypassed — and it reportedly can [CS: HIGH].
