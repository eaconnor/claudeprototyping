# Cloud Commander 2024 UX Research Strategy — Business Objectives

**Source:** Confluence — Cloud Commander - 2024 UX Research Strategy

## Company Objectives

- Generate $2.7M in Cloud Commander ARR

## TPG Objectives

1. 1200 MSPs using Cloud Commander
2. Self service onboarding by 50% of population
3. Stickiness 30%+ (MAU/DAU)
4. Generate 20 blog/SM posts and 30 Boot Camps on Cloud

## UX Objectives

(Same as TPG Objectives, plus:)
- Deliver a product experience that is intuitive and user friendly
- Understand the key features and functionalities partners need to see to find value in Cloud Commander
- Develop features that satisfy the business needs of MSP/CSPs
