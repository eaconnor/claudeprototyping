## Overview
Kandji is a modern Apple MDM platform built for security-conscious enterprise and mid-market organizations, with growing MSP channel adoption. It differentiates through a library of pre-built compliance blueprints (CIS, NIST, SOC 2, CMMC), automated remediation, and Liftoff — a zero-touch Mac onboarding experience. Kandji is gaining MSP channel traction as an alternative to Jamf, particularly following uncertainty around Jamf's Francisco Partners acquisition.

## MSP Relevance
Kandji is relevant to MSPs serving enterprise clients with significant Apple fleets and compliance requirements. Its pre-built compliance blueprints reduce the manual effort of configuring security policies from scratch. The MSP channel program is growing, and Kandji is increasingly positioned as the modern, security-first Jamf alternative for MSPs who want Apple MDM with built-in compliance automation.

## Key Offerings & Capabilities
- Apple MDM — macOS, iOS, iPadOS, tvOS management
- Pre-built compliance blueprints — CIS, NIST, SOC 2, CMMC, and custom baselines, auto-enforced
- Automated remediation — detects and corrects drift from compliance baselines without manual intervention
- Liftoff — zero-touch Mac onboarding and enrollment experience
- Library of pre-built app and configuration catalog items
- Endpoint security — integrated EDR-lite capabilities alongside MDM
- MSP multi-tenant management portal

## Recent Announcements
- Continuing to expand compliance blueprint library — adding new frameworks (CMMC, industry-specific)
- Growing MSP channel program with dedicated partner resources
- Positioned as primary beneficiary of uncertainty from Jamf's Francisco Partners acquisition
- Apple DDM (Declarative Device Management) support being added as Apple shifts management framework
- Continued investment in automated remediation as core differentiator

## Pricing Model
Per-device per-year subscription. Pricing starts around $8–10/device/year for basic MDM. Pricing transparent on website for standard tiers. Enterprise and MSP pricing requires sales engagement. Generally priced similarly to or slightly above Jamf for comparable feature sets.

## Strengths
- Pre-built compliance blueprints with automated remediation — the strongest compliance automation of any Apple MDM
- Modern UI — cleaner and faster than Jamf Pro
- Liftoff zero-touch onboarding reduces MSP deployment time significantly
- Security-first architecture appeals to compliance-heavy clients
- Gaining MSP channel momentum as Jamf's direction becomes uncertain under private equity

## Weaknesses / Gaps
- Smaller ecosystem and community than Jamf (Jamf Nation is massive)
- Less mature in larger, more complex enterprise environments than Jamf Pro
- Apple-only — irrelevant for cross-platform or Windows-heavy MSP environments
- MSP program still maturing relative to Jamf's more established channel

## Competitive Threat to NZO
Level: Low
Reason: Kandji focuses on Apple MDM with compliance automation. No meaningful overlap with NZO's AI agent platform. Kandji's automated remediation is compliance-scoped (MDM policy drift), not broader IT workflow automation.

## Notes
- Jamf acquisition by Francisco Partners (October 2025) is creating a real window for Kandji in the MSP market
- Compliance blueprint automated remediation is a strong MSP value proposition for clients in regulated industries
- CMMC compliance blueprint is strategically important for defense-adjacent MSPs
