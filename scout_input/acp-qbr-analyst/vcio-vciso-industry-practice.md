# vCIO / vCISO Industry Practice — Scout Ingredients
Scouted 2026-07-06. Ingredients only, no synthesis. One claim per line. All figures directional; most vendor-published — see [WOBBLY] flags.

## 1. vCIO — definition & deliverables
- vCIO = outsourced/contracted senior IT strategy role, delivered remotely; aligns IT with business goals, budgeting, roadmapping. [CS: HIGH] https://www.itglue.com/blog/vcio-services/
- Typical deliverables: technology roadmap (updated quarterly), IT budget planning, QBRs, compliance alignment (HIPAA/SOC 2/PCI/CMMC), on-demand advisory, vendor management. [CS: HIGH] https://www.gxait.com/blog/vcio-services-guide
- Positioned as senior tech leadership "at a fraction of the cost of a full-time hire." [CS: HIGH] https://www.inscnet.com/blog/what-is-a-vcio-how-msps-provide-virtual-cio-services-to-smbs/
- Delivered as a premium tier within an MSP engagement. [CS: HIGH] https://superops.com/blog/what-does-being-vcio-mean

## 2. vCISO — definition, deliverables, platforms
- vCISO = CISO expertise as a service for SMBs without adding headcount/cost. [CS: HIGH] https://cynomi.com/blog/what-is-a-vciso-platform-and-where-should-you-start/
- Deliverables: compliance readiness assessments, security-awareness training plans, risk/gap analysis, security policy authoring, remediation plans. [CS: HIGH]
- Cynomi is one AI-powered vCISO platform (vendor; self-stats vendor-published). [CS: HIGH / WOBBLY on self-stats] https://cynomi.com/
- Cynomi reframing category as "Security Growth Platform." [CS: MEDIUM] https://thehackernews.com/2026/06/the-security-growth-platform-why-msps.html
- Named vCISO competitors (Vanta, Drata, Apptega, ConnectSecure, CyberCNS) NOT confirmed this search. [CS: UNKNOWN] — Beth verify

## 6. Is the category "lifecycle management"? (frame test — Beth's observation)
- "Lifecycle management" = ScalePad's frame, rooted in asset-lifecycle origins; now rebranding toward "Customer Success platform." [CS: HIGH] https://www.scalepad.com/lifecycle-manager · https://msp.toolsinfo.com/tool/scalepad-lifecycle-manager-x
- "Technology alignment" / "Technology Success" = TruMethods/myITprocess frame — Standards, Alignment, Impact, Strategy; the "Technology Alignment Manager (TAM)" role. Distinct lineage. [CS: HIGH] https://www.trumethods.com/framework · https://help.myitprocess.kaseya.com/help/Content/standards-alignment-compliance/TAM_Training_Manual.pdf
- "vCIO" itself = a third frame (the role, not a process). [CS: HIGH] https://www.itglue.com/blog/vcio-services/
- "Customer Success" = fourth frame, increasingly used; tied to MRR/retention. [CS: MEDIUM] https://medhacloud.com/blog/managed-services-market-statistics-2026
- FRAME TEST RESULT: "lifecycle management" reads as **ScalePad-specific vendor framing, NOT the dominant industry-wide frame**; competing frames (tech alignment / vCIO / customer success) coexist. [CS: MEDIUM — frame-origin tracing]

## WOBBLY note
