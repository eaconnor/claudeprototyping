# Cloud Commander 2024 UX Research Strategy — Known Unknowns

**Source:** Confluence — Cloud Commander - 2024 UX Research Strategy

## Things that we do not know (per document)

1. Do partners prefer subject-based navigation or action-based navigation?
2. What are the most important settings that partner monitor on a regular basis?
3. What data do partners find the most valuable illustrated in a report?
4. What are the top-tasks that partners perform on a regular basis?
5. What do partners need to see to feel that their customers' environments are sufficiently secured
6. How do partners expect to manage licenses and subscriptions?
7. What type of automation and standardization do partners currently have established?

## Corresponding learning objectives

Document lists matching objectives to explore each unknown:
- Explore how partners expect to navigate a Cloud management solution
- Identify the top setting partners monitor on a regular basis
- Identify what data do partners find the most valuable illustrated in a report
- Explore the top-tasks that partners perform on a regular basis
- Understand how partners currently manage licenses and subscriptions
- Understand what type of automation and standardization partners currently have

## Note

These are the intended research questions. No findings are provided in this strategy document.
