# Methods Library Matrix v2

## Lineage Note — the Spine

The rubric's spine is the **IIT Institute of Design design-as-science tradition** [R — Bauhaus (Moholy-Nagy, New Bauhaus Chicago, 1937) → IIT Institute of Design → Doblin / Whitney / Kumar / Owen / Keeley genealogy]. The lineage's commitments shape the rubric structurally:

- **Bauhaus rigor** — falsifiable, iterative, repeatable methods. "Science" in this lineage means *rigor*, not 19th-century positivism.
- **Mies "less is more"** — aesthetic discipline; bury theory, surface process.
- **"300 prototypes > 1 genius"** — iteration over inspiration. Canvas-as-primary follows directly.
- **"We do things together"** — collaborative authorship. Forced-rank and Hard Mode are done with the team.
- **IDEO as side** — human-centered prototyping layered on Bauhaus rigor. IDEO Design Thinking sits in META alongside Owen two-step development.

Methods from outside the IIT lineage (STRAT, INNOV, PSYCH, UX, MKT, ABD, CMPLX, IDEO, PLAN, ANT) are absorbable operations — the IIT spine was built to integrate them, not compete with them [R — Owen Metaplanning].

## The Governing Principle: Innovation Navigates Paradox

There are no conflicts in this methods library. Methods that *appear* to conflict (Maslow vs. SDT, JTBD vs. need-theory, Porter vs. Blue Ocean, Lean Startup vs. Owen Structured Planning, IDEO Design Thinking vs. Cynefin) do not contradict — they **cluster around different questions** [R — abductive analysis (Tavory/Timmermans): alternative readings are parallel hypotheses, not opponents].

The rubric never forces the PM to choose a camp. It surfaces the cluster whose question matches the moment, and lets adjacent clusters be visited when the moment shifts. The PM holds the paradox; the rubric supports the holding [R — IIT tradition built to absorb across schools without forcing reconciliation; the operator's framing: *"innovation navigates paradox"*, session 2026-05-21].

Operational consequence: **RELATN scores in this library are bounded `0` to `+2`.** No negative scores. Methods either reinforce each other (same engine or complementary) or are independent (different cluster, different question). They never conflict.

## The Item × Item RELATN Matrix (Methods × Methods) — Adjacency List

**Format note (rewrite history):** An earlier version of this artifact attempted a printed 44×44 ASCII grid. The grid was hand-built with inconsistent column widths and the diagonal drifted past row 5, making off-diagonal positions unreliable. - Each method's entry lists its **+2** (strong reinforce — same engine) and **+1** (weak reinforce — complementary) partners.

## The Rubric as Band of Methods

Each cluster is a **voice** carrying its tradition's epistemology. When the PM works on an idea, the tool surfaces methods from the relevant clusters and each method **scores itself** — runs Owen Structured Planning on its own contribution:
- Names its own Sign / Hypothesis / Kill criterion
- States its force level (Must / Should / Ought to)
- Cites its evidence and tags its confidence [CS:]
- Acknowledges what it cannot see

The PM does not pick winners. The PM reads the transcript and finds the innovative space in the tension between the voices. *Paradox is the source of innovation, not a problem to resolve* [R — the operator's framing 2026-05-21; The Strategic Prototyping Protocol's own structure: many voices in productive tension serving the operator, not the vendor].
