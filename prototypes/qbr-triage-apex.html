Triage axes — the three questions
1
Risk to the MSP

If this client gets breached and we missed a known hole — is that on us?

Signals to watch Unaddressed findings from prior EBR · known documented exposure · high-sensitivity data (legal, financial, health, NGO with named targets)
2
Risk to the Client

How bad is their actual exposure right now? Could they survive a breach?

Signals to watch Inforcer alignment score (1–9) · phishing fail rate · end-of-life hardware · MFA gaps · unpatched or off-policy tenant · DLP / retention gaps
3
Growth Opportunity

Is there a clear next step that improves their security AND generates revenue?

Signals to watch On-prem moving to cloud · Business Standard → Premium gap · missing SOC / Usecure / Cyber Essentials · upsell path is clear and documented
