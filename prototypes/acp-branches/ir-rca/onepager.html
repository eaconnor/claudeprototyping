1

The wedge is timing, not technology

"Doing the RCA during the incident is equivalent to helping resolve it." — MSP, source interview

Move the same report from after the incident to during it, and it stops being a synthesis exercise and becomes part of the response a client is paying for. That timing shift is the entire differentiation case.

Trust mechanism
2

The buyer already has a reason to pay

MSPs are already trying to prove containment — to a client, an auditor, and an insurer. The evidence for that proof exists today, but it's scattered across six tools and three threads, and a defensible version of it doesn't exist until days later, if at all. This driver ranks #1 on cyber-insurance policy requirements — it isn't a hypothesis about future demand.

Compliance-driven demand
3

Two outputs, one gate — how it earns trust

The coworker produces two calibrated outputs: a technical evidence chain for the team, and a client/insurer narrative for the outside world — each claim tagged by confidence, not stated as flat fact. Nothing reaches a client or insurer without a human review gate. That gate is the liability protection, not a formality; skip it and the report stops being a trust artifact.

4

N-able's actual advantage: owning the log at the bottom

Adlumin's live threat telemetry, plus the SOC's large auto-remediation corpus, is what makes a root cause credible in context — a signal only means something if the coworker already understands what similar signals meant before. Whoever owns the log at the bottom of the evidence chain owns the margin on this category. If it's Adlumin-native, that's N-able.

Chokepoint argument
The ask

Answer Gate C first — before any further design spend on this package.

Confirm whether cross-tenant Adlumin incident data is technically and legally reachable by a coworker, or whether Adlumin sits in a separate product lane. That answer is binary and sits upstream of everything else in this concept — get it from Legal and Adlumin engineering, not from a design review.

Read before you get excited This concept survives Idea Chess screening at ~0.55% naive — it dies at the Build stage, and the failure mode isn't the confidence-tag UI (that's solvable). It's whether the evidence chain exists at all. Gate C — Adlumin incident data access, technical and legal, cross-tenant — is currently unconfirmed, and live zero-holes cross-stack correlation is rated only ~30% feasible today. Separately, a wrong root cause delivered with false confidence is an asymmetric, unrecoverable risk for the MSP and for N-able — that risk does not shrink just because the pitch above reads clean. The "10–15% of SOC running expense" and per-incident pricing framing mentioned in early interviews is single-source [CS: MEDIUM] and should not be treated as a market number.
