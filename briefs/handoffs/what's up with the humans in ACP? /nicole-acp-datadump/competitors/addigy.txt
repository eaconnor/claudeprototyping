## Overview
Addigy is an Apple MDM platform purpose-built for MSPs, offering multi-tenant macOS and iOS management with per-technician pricing rather than per-device. It differentiates from Jamf and Kandji by being explicitly MSP-native — built from the ground up for multi-client management, with a partner-first pricing model and MSP-friendly deployment workflows. Addigy serves smaller-to-mid-market MSPs managing Apple-heavy client environments.

## MSP Relevance
Addigy is the most MSP-native Apple MDM platform available. Its per-technician pricing removes the per-device cost escalation that Jamf and Kandji create for MSPs with large Apple fleets. Multi-tenant management is built-in rather than bolted on. For MSPs growing their Apple practice, Addigy provides a simpler, more economical path than enterprise-focused competitors.

## Key Offerings & Capabilities
- Apple MDM — macOS, iOS, iPadOS management, multi-tenant
- Per-technician pricing — unlimited devices per technician license
- Policy management and configuration profiles
- App deployment and patch management
- Automated remediation workflows
- Integrations with PSA tools (ConnectWise, Autotask, HaloPSA)
- Remote access and screen sharing for Mac
- Compliance monitoring and reporting

## Recent Announcements
- Ongoing feature development with Apple DDM (Declarative Device Management) support
- Integration ecosystem expanding with additional PSA and RMM connections
- Positioned as the MSP-native Apple MDM alternative as Jamf's direction shifts under Francisco Partners
- Continued focus on per-technician pricing as differentiator against per-device competitors

## Pricing Model
Per-technician per-month, unlimited devices. Pricing around $25–50/technician/month (varies by tier). No per-device fees. MSP partner pricing available. More economical than Jamf or Kandji for MSPs managing large Apple device counts per technician.

## Strengths
- MSP-native architecture — built for multi-client management from day one
- Per-technician, unlimited-device pricing is the most MSP-friendly model in Apple MDM
- Simpler deployment and configuration than Jamf Pro
- PSA integrations connect Apple management into MSP billing and ticketing workflows
- Responsive to MSP community feedback

## Weaknesses / Gaps
- Smaller brand recognition than Jamf in enterprise and larger organizations
- Less mature compliance automation than Kandji
- Fewer pre-built compliance blueprints than Kandji or Jamf Pro
- Limited AI or automation features compared to cross-platform RMM tools

## Competitive Threat to NZO
Level: Low
Reason: Addigy focuses on Apple MDM — a specialized, cross-platform management niche. No meaningful overlap with NZO's AI agent and workflow automation platform.

## Notes
- Per-technician, unlimited-device pricing is a strong MSP-specific differentiator — Addigy and Atera both win on this model
- Jamf's private equity acquisition creates an opening for Addigy among MSPs seeking a stable, MSP-native Apple MDM
- Worth tracking as Apple device adoption continues to grow in enterprise — the Apple MDM market will expand
