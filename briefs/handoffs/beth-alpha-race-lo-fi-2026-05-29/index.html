INC-2026-0847 Critical In Progress
What happened
On May 28, 2026 at 14:23 UTC, endpoint ACME-WS-0412 triggered multiple alerts for credential dumping activity. Mimikatz.exe was executed from a user's Downloads folder, followed by LSASS memory access and attempted lateral movement to DC01. The user account (jdoe@acme.local) showed no previous security incidents.
What we did
Alpha isolated ACME-WS-0412 at 14:24 UTC (automated). SOC analyst verified isolation and disabled jdoe@acme.local in Active Directory at 14:31 UTC. We reviewed authentication logs for the past 72 hours and identified no successful lateral movement beyond initial reconnaissance. Forensic image captured for deeper analysis.
What to do next
Confirm whether jdoe downloaded Mimikatz intentionally (insider threat) or if workstation was compromised prior to credential dump (phishing/malware). Review email logs for jdoe from May 25-28. Scan all domain controllers for persistence mechanisms (scheduled tasks, WMI, registry autoruns). Consider re-imaging ACME-WS-0412 rather than remediation.
