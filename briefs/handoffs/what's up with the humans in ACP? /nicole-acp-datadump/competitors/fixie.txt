## Overview
Fixie.ai is a voice AI platform founded in 2022 by former Google engineers (Matt Welsh, Zach Koch, Justin Uberti — creators of Chrome and WebRTC technologies), providing a cloud-based platform for building, hosting, and scaling conversational voice AI agents with real-time speech processing. The company has evolved from a general AI agent platform toward specialization in enterprise voice-based automation, with a June 2025 partnership with Hexaware Technologies for enterprise voice operations.

## MSP Relevance
Fixie.ai has minimal direct MSP relevance. It targets a narrow voice AI niche focused on enterprise operations rather than managed services or IT automation. Its private pricing model, limited channel presence, and absence of MSP-specific tooling make it unsuitable for typical MSP service offerings. MSPs specializing in voice AI implementations or enterprise conversational interfaces might evaluate Fixie.ai, but it is not a mainstream MSP platform.

## Key Offerings & Capabilities
- Voice AI platform — real-time speech processing and transcription for enterprise applications
- Conversational agent builder — natural language agents designed to answer questions and take autonomous actions via voice
- Tool and API integration — agents connect to external tools, data sources, and APIs via voice-triggered commands
- Business process automation — agents function as autonomous systems within enterprise voice workflows
- Enterprise operations focus — particularly for customer-facing voice automation and employee-facing voice-driven IT requests

## Recent Announcements
- June 2025: Partnership with Hexaware Technologies to power voice AI for enterprise operations — primary public announcement of 2025
- No Series A or follow-on funding announced since $17M Series Seed (March 2023, led by Redpoint Ventures)
- Company operating in stealth mode relative to broader AI agent market — minimal public communication
