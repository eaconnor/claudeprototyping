## Three Psychological Needs

Self-Determination Theory says people are motivated when they feel:
1. **Autonomous** (in control of their choices)
2. **Competent** (capable and effective)
3. **Related** (connected to others or a broader purpose)

Your product should support all three, or explicitly choose which to prioritize.

---

## Autonomy (Does the user feel in control?)

**Questions to ask:**
- Does the product make decisions for the user or with the user?
- Can the user override or customize behavior?
- Is the approval threshold explicit or hidden?

**Example (ACP):** "If ACP auto-executes runbooks without approval, MSP tech loses control → resistance. If ACP requires approval for every action, it's a recommendation engine, not an agent → no efficiency gain."

- Does it teach the user or replace them?
- What happens when the product encounters an edge case outside its training?

**Example (ACP):** "If ACP explains why it recommended an action (Trust Ledger as learning tool), tech competence increases. If ACP just shows what it did, tech becomes a button-pusher."
