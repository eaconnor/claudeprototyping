Council Testimony

Jobs-to-Be-Done: MSPs may be hiring for permission ("tell me it's safe") not comprehension ("explain the math"). Showing calculation might be overservice.

Service Blueprint: Calculation may only be relevant at specific moments (investigating failure). Should be hidden by default, surfaced on-demand.

Self-Determination Theory: View-only risks pseudo-autonomy (feels like surveillance). MSPs should be able to tune thresholds to support true control.

Disruptive Innovation: Sustaining feature for existing market, not disruptive. Risk of over-serving customer with unwanted sophistication.

Value Chain: Adlumin controls trust calculation as platform chokepoint. Making thresholds configurable surrenders control, risks fragmentation.
