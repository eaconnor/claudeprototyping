## SECTION 01 — Vision, Strategy & ICPs

### Customer Outcome | Working Hypothesis
- **Killer outcome:** Consolidate endpoint operations into a single AI-native platform that reduces effort, increases technician productivity, improves cyber-resilience. "Land and Retain hit the company OKRs."
- **Working hypothesis:** The killer app remains stable; our ability to deliver it strengthens. Destination is consolidation; entry point is operational value.
- **4 phases:**
  1. Launch IT Pro — 100–299 FTE IT orgs. Operational wedge: patch, vuln, endpoint ops, AI Coworkers, fast onboarding. May coexist with other tools. Outcome: less effort, faster remediation.
  2. Expand IT + enter MSP — 300–999 FTE IT + 1–25 FTE MSPs. Add endpoint workflows, MSP ops, broader Coworker coverage. Outcome: fewer tools, more automation.
  3. 25–50 FTE MSPs + larger/sophisticated IT — governance, compliance, policy, integrations, cyber-resilience. Outcome: more ops through one platform.
  4. All new MSP + IT Pro acquisition — realise platform vision, open ecosystem across both GTM motions.
- **Important nuance:** Phase 1 need not replace every incumbent tool; solve a valuable problem well enough to earn adoption, then consolidate.
- **Mackie margin note:** "Is this strong enough…? cyber security & resilience — observe and respond • Full endpoint data & execute (our agent secret sauce) • Patching, vuln, scripting • AI co-worker powered. Super automated"

### Execution Strategy | Phased GTM
- **Winning formula:** Win through simplicity. Differentiate through AI and customer cyber-resilience outcomes. Expand through openness. Scale through platform leverage.
- Phase 1 why-win: Ninja-level simplicity + time to value; AI-powered trial UX; net-new standalone; patch/vuln-led wedge; AI Coworkers from launch.
- Phase 2: expand Coworker coverage; MSP multi-tenancy; open GraphQL/MCP; "No Classic N-sight dependency for new NCA customers; N-central unchanged."
- Phase 3: preserve simplicity as capability expands; deeper automation, policy, governance; compliance/cyber-resilience.
- Phase 4: default to Ecoverse across both motions.

### ICP Definition — NinjaOne proven vs. proposed AI-era
- **NinjaOne proven wedge:** best fit growth-stage MSPs ~11–50 employees; professionalising ops, limited platform-eng capacity; fast deployment, tool consolidation; simplicity over configurability; land simple RMM then expand.
- **Ecoverse proposed wedge:** owner-led/early-management MSPs 1–49 employees, SMB fully-managed. Core fit 1–15 employees, generalist techs. Growth fit 10–49. Initial value = AI-guided/Coworker automation on core RMM. Expansion = open GraphQL/MCP.
- **Framing:** "How do we re-run the Ninja playbook in the AI era?"

## SECTION 02 — Competitive Landscape

### Working Framework (competitor set)
- **Primary benchmarks:** NinjaOne, Action1, Microsoft Intune, ManageEngine Endpoint Central, Automox
- **UEM-focused:** Hexnode, Ivanti, Omnissa Workspace ONE, Jamf
- **AI/emerging watch list:** Flamingo, Atera, SuperOps
- **Enterprise boundary:** Tanium, CrowdStrike, SentinelOne

### Primary Benchmarks — how each pitches internal IT
- NinjaOne: simplicity benchmark. Action1: patching/remediation, security-focused, lightweight. Intune: Microsoft-native control plane. ManageEngine: breadth-and-value. Automox: cloud-native automation specialist.
- Shared persona: resource-constrained internal IT team.

### Action1 benchmark (proof of sub-1,000-endpoint wedge)
- $12.7M ARR (2025) +76% YoY; 275% YoY six-figure deal ARR; 98% retention; 5,000+ customers / 10M+ endpoints; Inc. 5000.
- Self-serve, IT-Pro-direct, not MSP-first. Free tier below 200 endpoints, no feature limits. Positions vs WSUS/Intune/SCCM/ManageEngine/NinjaOne sprawl.
- **Open question:** "If we pursue the same segment, our USP must be what it does NOT provide: broader UEM/RMM, AI UI/Coworkers, open platform."

### Flamingo benchmark (open-source, AI-native, cheap)
- 298 customers from 1,000-MSP waitlist; $1/device/mo Gen1 (→$3 Gen2 →$5 Gen3); Focal VC, Array VC, Vertex Ventures; launched from stealth Oct 2025.
- Self-serve MSP+MSSP direct, "replaces your stack"; 14-day trial no card; open-source core + managed SaaS, no-lock-in.
- Gen1: RMM, remote access, patching, PSA, SIEM, MDM, docs. Two AI agents (Fae tickets, Mingo ops). Gen2/3 roadmap adds backup, EDR/EPP, IAM, email security, ZTNA, XDR, MDR.
- **Open question:** "Flamingo competes on price and openness, not depth. Our seam is AI UI/Coworker depth, governance, N-able trust and scale, not matching price."
