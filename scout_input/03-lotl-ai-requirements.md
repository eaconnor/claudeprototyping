## Why AI Is Required (Not Optional)

Rule-based and human-scale security cannot detect LOTL attacks because:

- LOTL attacks use valid credentials  
- They use legitimate tools  
- They move slowly  

**Signal exists only statistically.** [CS: VERIFIED]

---

## What AI Must Do

AI is required to:

1. **Learn "normal" at massive scale**  
   [CS: VERIFIED]

2. **Detect drift, not signatures**  
   [CS: VERIFIED]

3. **Correlate behavior over time that no human could connect manually**  
   [CS: VERIFIED]

---

## The UX Problem

Poorly designed AI:

- Creates alert fatigue  
- Erodes trust  
- Gets disabled or ignored  

**This is a UX problem, not a model problem.** [CS: VERIFIED]
