Prep Room

QBR + vCIO · the capstone · MVP FIRST
The coworker drafts the whole quarterly review; the MSP walks in, edits, and owns it.
  • Draft-first — assembled from the quarter, not a blank page
  • Review & edit before send — the MSP is the author
  • Right info at the right moment — client + meeting date aware
  • Gaps surface — "here's what I couldn't normalize"

Two Voices

audience-adaptive · vCISO ↔ vCIO · same facts, two tellings
One set of evidence, told two ways — the risk story for the security buyer, the business story for the exec.
  • Toggle the audience — technical board vs. business owner
  • Same ledger, re-voiced — not two data pulls, one truth
  • MSP is the hero — the coworker never fronts the client
  • Predict what lands — MSP tunes for their client

Strategy Layer

forward-looking · vCIO planning · the SHOULD tenet

Evidence Locker

IR RCA + vCISO · compliance driver · MVP SECOND
Every incident writes its own chain of evidence as it happens — RCA that's already assembled when the auditor (or insurer) asks.
  • Auto chain-of-evidence — captured in real time, not after
  • Two outputs — technical RCA + compliance/insurance pull
  • Read + write-with-approval — rollback always on the table
  • Cross-stack correlation — the moat, and the risk

Coworker Handoff

earned autonomy · gated by default · the trust UX
The coworker reasons to the fix, then hands off to validated code — and graduates from "human confirms" to "AI executes, human audits" as it earns it.
  • Reason → substitute — stops spending tokens once the action is deterministic Substitution Engine (VERIFIED)
  • Gated autonomy by default — approval before risky writes
  • Show its work — the reasoning is legible, not a black box
  • Earns its dial — track record unlocks scope over time
