## Core positioning

N-able's brand positioning is engineered to be the **MSP's undisputed 'expert behind the expert,'** a strategy built upon the foundational pillars of security, reliability, and partnership. [CS: MEDIUM — secondary source interpretation]

This focus empowers managed service providers to grow their businesses securely and profitably, transforming the platform into a genuine strategic advantage. [CS: MEDIUM]

## Core message

The core promise is to empower MSPs serving SMBs with **enterprise-level security capabilities that are both accessible and manageable**. [CS: HIGH — reflected in homepage messaging]

**Three key differentiators:**
1. Enterprise-grade security packaged for the MSP and SMB market
2. Unwavering platform reliability proven through independent industry surveys
3. A genuine partnership model that integrates with the MSP's operations

[CS: MEDIUM — derived from marketing analysis]

## Target market

N-able's primary customer segments are exclusively **Managed Service Providers** operating within a B2B2X model. The core target is the MSP business owner or technical decision-maker, typically aged 35-55, whose firm services small and medium-sized businesses. [CS: MEDIUM]

N-able leverages a hybrid sales model to capitalize on a global MSP market projected to exceed $500 billion by 2026. [CS: LOW — projection from secondary source]

## Product messaging (from homepage)

**N‑central®** — Security-first endpoint management platform built for MSPs, with autonomous operations driven by AI. Teams manage more clients, more endpoints, more services. [CS: VERIFIED — homepage copy]

**N‑able Managed Detection and Response** (powered by Adlumin) — 24/7 SOC coverage, with AI-driven detection, automated response, and human expert analysts working together. [CS: VERIFIED — homepage copy]

**Cove Data Protection** — Cloud-native backup built for MSP scale with no appliances, no babysitting, no surprises. [CS: VERIFIED — homepage copy]

## Industry recognition

Platform voted top RMM by CRN readers for three consecutive years (2021-2023). Supports 500,000+ endpoints globally. [CS: HIGH — verifiable claims]
