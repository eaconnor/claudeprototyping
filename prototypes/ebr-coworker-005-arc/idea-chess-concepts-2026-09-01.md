## VP-3 (carried forward, evolved) · BUSINESS RESILIENCE, PROVEN NOT JUST TOLD

*For MSP owners exposed to churn, consolidation, and PE-buyout pressure, EBR Coworker is an AI Coworker
that turns every EBR into a documented resilience and expansion touchpoint — surfacing cyber/SOC upsell
only from real, evidenced gaps and logging liability discipline automatically. Unlike a generic QBR
template, it builds the kind of written trail that let one UK MSP rebuild ~£300k in recurring revenue
after losing 16% of its book to a buyout — and this generation can back every line of that trail with a
visible, traceable source instead of asking the MSP to take the AI's word for it.*

**Earliest fall: survives to Defend, same as the original pass — but the grounding gap named then is
unchanged, not closed. [A: med-high].** The new traceability discipline (honesty badges, code-cited
facts) makes the *product's own conduct* match the *pitch's* evidentiary standard — a real coherence
gain. It does **not** touch the original weakness the 2026-08-20 pass already named: the £300k anchor is
still "n=1, vendor-selected" [CS: MEDIUM]. Conflating "the product is now more honest" with "the growth
story is now more proven" would be exactly the kind of confidence-inflation §4 exists to prevent — naming
the distinction here so it doesn't blur later.

## VP-4 (carried forward, strengthened) · YOUR JUDGMENT, MADE VISIBLE AND UN-COPYABLE

*For MSP delivery leads whose competitive edge is judgment they can't delegate, EBR Coworker is an AI
Coworker that carries that judgment forward as a governed, versioned runbook — showing its full 9-step
reasoning trail (continuity, gap-judgment, translation, chooser) so expertise compounds instead of living
in one person's head. Unlike a black-box report generator, the judgment stays inspectable and correctable
(teach-back) at every step.*

**Earliest fall: Adopt, down from the original pass's "supporting, reframe needed." [A: med].** The
original council flagged VP-4's danger as wording ("in every room" reads as capacity, one inch from the
killed time-saved frame) and rated it strong on defensibility but only medium on F8. This session's build
gives it something it never had: an actual artifact (the run-trace, the versioned config) instead of an
assertion. That should raise its confidence — but the same two showcase steps flagged in the (superseded)
implementation-level pass are still `⟨VERIFY⟩`: Continuity/delta and Judge-the-8-tabs. A delivery lead
being pitched "your judgment, made visible" who opens the trace and finds the two steps closest to actual
judgment still marked placeholder gets the opposite of the intended proof. **Promotion to co-headline
candidate is premature until those two steps carry real content, not just real UI.**

## VP-6 (new) · RADICAL HONESTY, AS THE PRODUCT

*For MSP technicians evaluating whether to trust an AI Coworker with client-facing deliverables, EBR
Coworker is an AI Coworker that visibly flags exactly which capabilities are real today versus still in
development — so nothing in it hides an integration gap. Unlike a vendor demo that shows only the
finished vision, EBR Coworker shows its own unfinished edges.*

**Earliest fall: Genesis — fails before it reaches a real buyer. [A: low].** This is the sharpest finding
in this pass. A buyer does not evaluate a vendor's own architecture maturity as a purchase criterion —
they evaluate outcomes. "We tell you what isn't built yet" is a genuine, real commitment (CLAUDE.md
§10b's Zero Vector honesty mandate, and Gate 2's "a prototype that implies capability that doesn't exist
is hallucination shipped as UI") — but that is a **governance obligation on the builder**, not a
**benefit the buyer is shopping for**. Stated as a Moore positioning statement, the target customer and
the benefit contradict each other: no MSP technician's stated need is "I want to know about your
backend's in-memory stubs." **This rhymes exactly with the original pass's VP-5 verdict** — "right design
principle, wrong headline" — but for a different reason: VP-5 over-claimed ("PROVEN") on thin grounding;
VP-6 under-claims by foregrounding the vendor's own incompleteness as if it were a feature. Both fail by
putting the vendor's posture where the buyer's outcome should be.

## VP-9 (new, previously implicit) · RICH SETUP AS THE DIFFERENTIATOR

*For MSP technicians overwhelmed by ad hoc EBR prep, EBR Coworker's real differentiator is the depth of
its setup wizard — multi-file ingestion, the Stefanie template, a primitives grid explaining the object
model, an intent-tier confidence table. Unlike lighter QBR generators, richness up front is the product.*

**Earliest fall: Build — doesn't survive being stated plainly enough to test. [A: low].** This bet was
never made on purpose; it accumulated from restoration decisions justified on lineage ("content confirmed
present there, absent everywhere after" [D]) rather than on a named technician need. The moment it's
written as a Moore statement, the contradiction is visible on its face: "more configuration steps than
competitors" is a feature-accumulation story, not a compelling reason to buy, and it directly conflicts
with this same project's own onboarding-speed instincts elsewhere in the corpus (the sibling coworker
build's ≤15-min/<20-min onboarding ceilings, `briefs/idea-chess-build.md` BET8). **The adversarial value
here isn't killing a stated pitch — it's exposing that a pitch was never stated, and that the unexamined
default (restore what existed) smuggled one in anyway.**
