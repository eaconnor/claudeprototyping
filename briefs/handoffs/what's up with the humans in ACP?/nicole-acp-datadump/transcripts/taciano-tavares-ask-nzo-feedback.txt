What Taciano Liked About Ask N‑zo
1. Real value from environment and device analysis
Taciano consistently highlighted value in Ask N‑zo’s ability to analyze devices and environments, especially for understanding current state and history.
On overall usefulness:
“The device performance evaluation. My first infrastructure analysis, configuration, inventory and categorisation, improvement recommendations for the environment, a history oriented analysis of managed device.”
 BUT WE ARE MISSING DATA, so not a huge win.  Rather we are 'directionally accurate"
 
2. Helpful for exploratory and scenario-based questions
His team used Ask N‑zo in an exploratory way, asking questions to better understand their environment or specific devices.
On how technicians used it:
“To discover more about our environment or a specific device.”
On usage style:
“That use and made a exploratory on scenario basis or a volume basis to measure these interactions.”
 
3. Fast enough to be operationally useful
Speed was explicitly called out as a positive. While not described as “zippy,” performance was good enough that it did not block usage.

“Not bad.”
