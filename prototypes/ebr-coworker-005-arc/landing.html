How it works

It learns your process. Not the other way around.

Most tools make you rebuild your workflow to fit the software. A coworker starts from the runbook you already use — so day one feels like handing work to someone who already knows how you do it.

01

You bring the runbook

Show it last quarter's report, or start from a template. It reads the structure — your sections, your sources, your grading — and drafts a coworker shaped like your work.

02

It does the legwork

It pulls from your stack, reconciles the mismatches, and drafts the parts that are drafts. The tedious hours before the meeting — done before you sit down.

03

You stay in charge

It proposes; you decide. It flags what it's guessing and holds back what it isn't sure of. Nothing reaches a client without a name on it.

Why you can trust it

It tells you what it knows — and what it's guessing.

A coworker that sounds equally sure of everything is one you have to double-check on everything. N-able coworkers grade their own confidence, in words, and hold back what they can't stand behind.

MEASURED

Pulled straight from a connected source. This is fact, and it says so.

BENCHMARKED

Compared against a standard or last quarter. Solid, with a stated basis.

ESTIMATED

Inferred — often because the data came in by hand. Flagged, never hidden.

UNVERIFIED

It won't guess. Held back from anything client-facing until a human vouches for it.
