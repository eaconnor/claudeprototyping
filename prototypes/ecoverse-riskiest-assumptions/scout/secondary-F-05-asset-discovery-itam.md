# F-05 — Asset Discovery, Inventory and ITAM at the Endpoint

Two different things get conflated here. Keeping them apart:

- **Asset discovery inside endpoint security** — sized only as a segment, by M&M, and it is the growth story on that axis.
- **IT Asset Management (ITAM)** — a separate, adjacent market with its own reports, lower growth, and a different buyer.

**Endpoint-scoped asset discovery as a standalone market: does not exist publicly.**

## Terminal findings

1. **"Endpoint asset discovery" is not a separately sized market.** It exists as a Type *inside* endpoint security (M&M) or as the adjacent ITAM market. No standalone report found. [CS: HIGH]
2. **M&M's 12.8% is the only public segment-level growth figure for asset discovery anywhere in this pass.** [CS: HIGH]
3. **ITAM and endpoint asset discovery are not interchangeable.** ITAM grows at ~6.3% (Mordor); asset discovery inside endpoint security grows at 12.8% (M&M). Different markets, different publishers, different definitions — **not a growth comparison, do not present it as one.** [CS: HIGH that both figures are as stated; the comparison is invalid]
4. **No UK or EU figure for either.** [CS: HIGH — negative finding] [THIN DOMAIN]
