## Overview
Todyl is an MSP-focused unified cybersecurity platform combining SASE, SIEM, endpoint security, MXDR (Managed XDR), SOAR, and GRC in a single platform. In 2026, Todyl launched Janus — a purpose-built agentic AI for security analysts that delivers in-case analysis via natural language. Todyl's vision is "unified assurance" — enabling MSPs to move beyond fragmented security tools toward a single platform covering prevention, detection, response, compliance, and insurability.

## MSP Relevance
Todyl is purpose-built for MSPs as a unified security platform, directly addressing MSP pain points around managing multiple security vendor relationships. The unified assurance narrative gives MSPs a compelling client conversation — covering prevention, detection, compliance, and cyber insurance in one platform. Janus (the agentic AI) is a direct investment in AI-driven security operations for MSP analysts.

## Key Offerings & Capabilities
- SASE — secure access service edge with ZTNA, cloud firewall, and secure web gateway
- Singularity SIEM — log management and threat detection
- Endpoint Security — AV, EDR capabilities
- MXDR — Managed Extended Detection and Response with 24/7 SOC
- SOAR — security orchestration, automation, and response
- GRC — governance, risk, and compliance management
- Janus Agentic AI — purpose-built AI for security analysts; natural language in-case analysis (2026)
- Todyl Spectra Certification Program — cyber insurance and risk management certification for MSP clients
- Three-tier packaging starting at $250/month platform subscription (September 2025)

## Recent Announcements
- Launched Janus, Todyl's agentic AI for security analysts (2026) — natural language in-case analysis for analysts of any experience level
- September 2025: Three-tier platform packaging launched at $250/month starting price
- January 2026: RSA partnership — RSA selected Todyl to protect its own environments and power its SOC
- Todyl Summit 2026: Platform vision keynote reinforcing commitment across SASE, SIEM, Endpoint, MXDR, SOAR, GRC
- Todyl Spectra Certification Program launched — cyber insurance certification that MSPs can offer to clients

## Pricing Model
Platform subscription starting at $250/month for the entry tier (September 2025 packaging). Per-endpoint and per-user components at higher tiers. MSP-specific pricing with partner discounts. Exact per-tier pricing requires contact with sales.

## Strengths
- Genuinely unified platform — one vendor for SASE, SIEM, MDR, SOAR, GRC instead of 5–6 point solutions
- Janus agentic AI is purpose-built for MSP security analysts, not a generic AI assistant
- Todyl Spectra creates a cyber insurance revenue stream for MSP partners
- RSA partnership validates enterprise credibility
- "Unified assurance" narrative is compelling for MSP client conversations

## Weaknesses / Gaps
- Smaller brand recognition than CrowdStrike, Sophos, or even Huntress
- Unified platform means each individual component may be less mature than best-of-breed alternatives
- Three-tier packaging (September 2025) is relatively new — market adoption still building
- SOAR and GRC are newer additions still proving out in production

## Competitive Threat to NZO
Level: Low-Medium
Reason: Todyl focuses on security unification, not MSP workflow automation. However, Janus (the agentic AI) is a purpose-built AI agent for MSP security workflows — conceptually adjacent to NZO's agentic automation. If Todyl extends Janus beyond security into broader MSP operations, the overlap increases.
