# Dossier: Liongard

## Correction to brief framing — acquisition direction is reversed
[CS: VERIFIED] **Liongard acquired DarkLight (Darklight Cyio), not the other way around.** The brief states "acquired by DarkLight" — this is backwards per every primary and secondary source found. Liongard remains the acquiring, independent company.
- Liongard's own press release: https://www.liongard.com/media-press/liongard-acquires-darklight-cyio/
- Businesswire (distributed press release): https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250606756080/en/Liongard-Advances-Proactive-Cybersecurity-with-Acquisition-of-Darklights-AI-Powered-Vulnerability-Prioritization-Platform
- MSSP Alert (trade press, independent writeup): https://www.msspalert.com/news/liongard-acquires-darklight-to-expand-ai-driven-vulnerability-prioritization-for-msps

## Date
[CS: VERIFIED] Announced June 6, 2025 (per Liongard's press release and Businesswire syndication). Crunchbase's acquisition record lists a close date of May 31, 2025.
- https://www.crunchbase.com/acquisition/liongard-acquires-champion-technology-company--65f8c93d
The brief's "May 2025" is close — plausibly referencing the close date rather than the public announcement date. Not a meaningful discrepancy, but worth noting the two dates differ by a week.

## Deal terms
[CS: VERIFIED — as "undisclosed"] No price or financial terms were disclosed in the press release. Company statements only characterize the deal as "accretive" and contributing "positively to earnings."
- https://www.liongard.com/media-press/liongard-acquires-darklight-cyio/

## What Liongard does
[CS: VERIFIED] Liongard describes itself (as of the June 2025 release) as an Attack Surface Management (ASM) platform for MSPs/MSSPs — "unified visibility across users, systems, networks, and cloud environments," 85+ integrations, asset intelligence, risk detection.
- https://www.liongard.com/media-press/liongard-acquires-darklight-cyio/
[CS: MEDIUM] Liongard's earlier public positioning (historically) was as an automated IT documentation / "digital footprint" tool for MSPs — closer to the "normalization" framing in the brief. The June 2025 messaging repositions the company toward ASM/cyber-risk, not documentation/normalization. This is a meaningful drift from what the brief seems to assume the company still is — worth Beth checking Liongard's current core product positioning directly rather than relying on older category memory.
