## What N-zo Can Do

### Analysis & Diagnostics
- Examines telemetry data (CPU, memory, disk usage) to troubleshoot device performance issues
- Scans asset data for outdated operating systems and security risks
- Analyzes patch logs to identify failure causes

### Information Retrieval
- Searches relevant product knowledge resources and summarizes recommendations
- Retrieves hardware specifications and asset details
- Compares device specs against compatibility requirements

### Reporting & Guidance
- Presents hardware summaries and asset health status
- Flags non-compliant devices and suggests remediation steps
- Provides filtered lists of eligible devices for onboarding

### Resource Linking
- Offers links to relevant content for manual troubleshooting
- Connects users to guides, examples, and reference materials

## Key Limitations

**N-zo does not execute autonomous actions.** [CS: VERIFIED]

Instead, it:
- Provides guidance and recommendations requiring human review
- Suggests next steps rather than implementing solutions
- Requires human approval for any actual remediation or changes

The platform functions as a diagnostic and informational tool, not an automated action executor.
