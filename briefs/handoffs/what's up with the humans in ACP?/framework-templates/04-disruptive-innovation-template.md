## Sustaining vs. Disruptive

**Sustaining innovations:** Make existing products better on dimensions incumbents already compete on (faster, more features, better quality on traditional metrics).

**Disruptive innovations:** Serve non-consumers or create new markets by offering "worse" performance on traditional metrics but better on a new dimension incumbents ignore (cheaper, simpler, more convenient).
