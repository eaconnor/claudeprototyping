## 1. THE 2.5× STAT — MISATTRIBUTION. Do not use in the QBR form.

Stefanie attributes to a 2022 Service Leadership report: *"MSPs that do make EBRs a regular component… experiencing on average two and a half times higher profitability."* `[boot camp 0:24:48]`

**The real Service Leadership 2.5× claim, verbatim:**
> "The top quartile SPs in each business model and in all geographies, earn about 2.5x the bottom line profit percent (EBITDA %) of their median peers, regardless of their size, age, owner compensation, client profile or vendor selections."

`[CS: VERIFIED — figure and language traceable]`
- https://itnation.connectwise.com/service-leadership/about-service-leadership-index
- https://service-leadership.com/products/msp-annual-industry-report.asp

**What it is actually about:** top-quartile vs median EBITDA discipline across 17 profitability metrics (gross margin by line of business, sales, marketing, G&A, fair-market-adjusted owner comp). **A whole-business-model comparison. Nothing to do with QBR cadence.**

**Checked and confirmed absent:**
- 2022 GlobeNewswire release announcing that year's profitability report — full text fetched, **zero** QBR/EBR/business-review language `[CS: VERIFIED absence]`
- ConnectWise SLI 2026 exec-summary page — no QBR link `[CS: VERIFIED absence on that page; full 400+pp report gated]`
- ConnectWise's own client-QBR best-practices blog — does not cite the 2.5× or any SLI data

**The likely source of the error — a genuine terminology collision:** Service Leadership/ConnectWise use "QBR" internally to mean *their analyst's* quarterly benchmarking review of an MSP's **own financials** against the Index — not the MSP's meeting with its client. See https://benchmarking.service-leadership.com/sli-qbrb-service `[CS: MEDIUM]`

**Verdict: `[CS: LOW — probable misattribution]`. The 2.5× figure is real; the causal link to client-facing EBRs is not in the record.** Full report is behind ConnectWise/Service Leadership purchase — N-able may have access internally.
