## Read
- **Resolves:** Fails to resolve. The switch trigger remains unanswered in the deck and unconfirmed in the wild — no external evidence found of MSPs switching to any competitor specifically for an AI-Coworker/execution-depth reason. The switching signal that does exist (pricing/support/PSA friction) is a *different* wedge than the one the deck proposes to open.
- **Riskiness:** Highest in the set. If wrong, the entire MSP GTM motion (Phases 2–4) has no answered reason-to-switch, and the company builds a platform nobody has a documented reason to move to. Compounding risk: Action1's new MSP program means the "MSP switch" lane the deck assumes is uncontested is now also being actively courted by an incumbent-adjacent competitor.
- **Test cost:** Cheap and fast relative to the stakes — direct switch-intent interviews with NinjaOne MSPs (the "IT LAG community" the deck already names as a research source), a Jobs-to-be-Done-style "what would make you fire Ninja" study. This is a research-design problem, not an engineering one; days-to-weeks, not a build cycle.
