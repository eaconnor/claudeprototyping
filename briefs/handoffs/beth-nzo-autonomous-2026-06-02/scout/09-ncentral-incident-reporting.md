**Retrieved:** 2026-06-02  
**Confidence:** [CS: VERIFIED] — Official product documentation

## Report Contents

The Incident Summary report in N-central displays incident data across three main sections:

### Summary Information
- Total number of incidents
- Average escalation times (first and second escalation)
- Average time to acknowledge notifications (first, second, third)
- Mean time to resolve incidents

### Detailed Incident Data
- When incidents occurred and closed
- Affected devices and services
- Service states
- Escalation timings
- Current status information

### Correlated Notifications
- When each correlated notification was triggered and closed
- Associated escalation and acknowledgment times

## Available Actions

Based on this report data, users can:
- Monitor incident frequency and patterns
- Analyze response times through escalation and acknowledgment metrics
- Track incident resolution efficiency
- Email or save the report for further distribution or record-keeping

**Note:** This report is accessible only at the Customer or Site organizational level.

---

**Observation:** Report provides data for analysis but does not trigger automated remediation. Human reviews data and decides on action.
