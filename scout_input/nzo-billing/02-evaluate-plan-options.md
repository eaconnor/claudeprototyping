## Study goals
Evaluate **three proposed upgrade workflows** for the N-zo AI credits feature; identify which best meets needs of users, sales, and support.

1. Which workflow is clearest for users to understand (what happens to plan, credits, billing at each stage).
2. Which workflow is easiest for sales to communicate and champion (reduces friction, supports conversion).
3. Surface workflow-specific risks / edge cases (surprise, unintended plan changes, misaligned billing expectations).
4. Provide a clear recommendation for which workflow to take forward.

**Out of scope:** does NOT evaluate credit tiers, pricing, or the feature itself — only upgrade and trial mechanics of the three workflows.

## The three workflows under test
- **Option 1 — Free till end of month:** free window on upgrade. Risk probed: customer times it to get free credits then reverts.
- **Option 2 — No trial, immediate:** immediate change, reversible. Risk probed: does "no trial" feel riskier to commit / do customers trust a reversible immediate change.
- **Option 3 — Standard auto-convert:** auto-renewal mechanics. Risk probed: trust vs. anxiety/churn.

## Script themes (sales-facing interviews)
- **What customers actually ask for** at the credit limit (more credits? upgrade? just stop?); common upgrade objections; revert cases; sensitivity to commitment (lock-in) vs. cost, by customer size.
- **Testing the three options:** can you explain it in a 2-min conversation; which generates fewest support escalations; which to reach for with a hesitant customer; which is most misunderstood after the fact.
- **Commercial/conversion angle:** per-option probes (above).
- **Workflow/process fit:** walk through mid-conversation without caveats; pipeline/forecast impact of provisional upgrades that revert; cleanest answer to "what happens to my credits if I upgrade mid-month?"
- **Blind spots:** customer-base factors none of the three account for (seasonal usage, multi-site, procurement cycles); which option to kill; top-5 accounts vs. price-sensitive accounts preference.

**Facilitation:** individual interviews (sales anchor on each other); show prototype per option before evaluation questions; separate "what I prefer to sell" from "what customers prefer to buy."
