## "Unicorn trend" — Wikipedia
Documents the 2010s unicorn-aesthetic revival as a named cultural/design phenomenon, with a described visual grammar: pastel gradients, holographic/iridescent finishes, glitter, high saturation pink/violet/baby-blue. Frames it explicitly as tied to 80s/90s childhood nostalgia.
[CS: MEDIUM — Wikipedia tier, useful as an entry point, not a primary or scholarly source]
Sources:
