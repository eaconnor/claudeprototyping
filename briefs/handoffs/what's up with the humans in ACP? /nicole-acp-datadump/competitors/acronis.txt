## Overview
Acronis Cyber Protect Cloud is an integrated backup, disaster recovery, and cybersecurity platform delivered exclusively through MSPs. It combines traditional BCDR with anti-malware, EDR, email security, and DLP in a single agent and management console. Acronis operates an MSP-first distribution model, selling only through service provider partners. In 2025, security integration adoption through the platform grew 123% YoY, signaling rapid platform expansion beyond core backup.

## MSP Relevance
Acronis is one of the most widely deployed backup platforms in the MSP market. Its single-agent approach covering both backup and security reduces the number of tools MSPs must manage. 300+ integrations and fast-growing XDR adoption make it a platform consolidator for MSPs seeking to reduce vendor count.

## Key Offerings & Capabilities
- Acronis Cyber Protect Cloud — backup, DR, anti-malware, EDR, URL filtering in one agent
- Cloud backup for physical, virtual, and cloud workloads
- Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace backup
- Managed Detection & Response (MDR) service tier
- Disaster recovery with cloud failover
- Email security and anti-phishing
- Vulnerability assessment and patch management
- AI assistant chat embedded in partner console (Jan 2025) — answers product and sales questions 24/7
- 300+ integrations with PSA, RMM, and security tools
- SLA reporting widgets for MDR — tracks MTTR, compliance trends, and configurable objectives

## Pricing Model
Per-workload and per-GB pricing through MSP partner tier. MSPs set their own markup and margin. Storage costs are separate from protection licensing. Tiered by feature set — Essentials (backup only) through Advanced (full cyber protection). No per-technician fees.

## Strengths
- Single agent for both backup and security — reduces endpoint footprint and complexity
- MSP-only distribution — no channel conflict with direct sales
- 300+ integrations make it easy to embed in existing MSP stacks
- Strong recognition in backup/DR segment (Canalys Champion two years running)
- Price-competitive for SMB workloads
- Growing MDR and XDR adoption signals platform maturation beyond backup

## Weaknesses / Gaps
- Security features less mature than dedicated EDR vendors (CrowdStrike, SentinelOne)
- Unified console can feel cluttered compared to single-purpose tools
- MDR service newer and less proven than Huntress or Blackpoint
- Perceived as a "backup company adding security" — limits credibility with security-first buyers

## Competitive Threat to NZO
Level: Low
Reason: Acronis competes in backup/DR with adjacent security. AI features are assistant-level (chatbot in console), not agentic workflow automation. Low direct overlap with NZO's core automation and agent platform play, though MSPs consolidating on Acronis reduce budget available for additional tools.
