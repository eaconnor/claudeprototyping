Palette — two distinct eras, don't blend them

Research found these as genuinely different: 80s-original was neon, not pastel. The soft pastel-iridescent look is the modern revival's palette (Starbucks 2017, Too Faced 2018), not the original Lisa Frank/Care Bears one. CS: HIGH both exist; CS: LOW that today's soft-pastel is period-accurate to the 80s itself.

Translation check — where #9/9b already land

The glam logo direction sent earlier used the modern-revival pastel + holographic sweep combination (Too Faced-style), not 80s-original neon. That's a deliberate, defensible choice — the neon-original palette (top swatch row above) would likely fight the app's existing serious-UI purple/cyan tokens much harder than the softer iridescent revival palette does.

Named gaps — don't treat this board as fully verified: "unicorn shampoo" as a specific 80s product wasn't confirmed by search (only a modern one exists) — if you have an exact name, say it and it can be re-checked. Packaging-level detail for My Little Pony and Barbie perfume (exact bottle colors/materials) is thin — mostly nostalgia-blog description, not archival photography. If pixel-accurate period reference matters, that needs an image search pass, not text research.
