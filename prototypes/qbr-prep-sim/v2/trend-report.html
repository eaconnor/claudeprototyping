Trend 04 · threat

Phishing against accountancy practices clusters around filing deadlines

What we're seeing

Industry benchmark reporting describes a seasonal pattern: lure emails impersonating HMRC, clients, and payroll spike in the fortnights before filing deadlines, when accounts teams are busiest and most likely to click. The attacker's calendar is your calendar.

For a practice like yours

Your riskiest inbox weeks are the same weeks your team can least afford a bad click. Awareness refreshers land best in the quiet month before the season, not during it.

Trend 05 · lifecycle

The Windows 10 end-of-support tail is driving replacement budgets

What we're seeing

Per Microsoft lifecycle announcements, Windows 10's end of support has left a long tail of machines that still work but no longer receive fixes. SMB practices across the cohort are converting that tail into phased replacement budgets rather than emergency purchases.

For a practice like yours

A machine out of support is a machine out of your insurer's good graces and out of security fixes — the practices handling this well are budgeting the replacements across quiet quarters, keeping busy season untouched.
