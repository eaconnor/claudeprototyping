## Source Description

Primary artifact: a PDF reproduction of the 2008/2009 Airbnb ("AirBed & Breakfast") seed pitch deck, hosted on an NC State University design-course site, self-identified on its cover slide as "a PowerPoint reproduction of an early AirBnB pitch deck via Business Insider" (linking to BI's 2011 article). Read directly, page by page.

Every slide is watermarked "Template by PitchDeckCoach.com," indicating it is a third-party recreation rather than the founders' original file.

- **Five slides verified verbatim:** Problem, Solution, Market Validation, Market Size, and Business Model cross-match independent secondary transcriptions (mypitchdecks.com, search-cached Business Insider summaries) word for word — high confidence these preserve the real original text.
- **Two slides demonstrably fabricated/reconstructed:** the Product slide shows a modern post-rebrand airbnb.com UI that did not exist in 2008–2009; the Competitive Advantages slide's body copy is literal "Lorem ipsum" Latin placeholder text under otherwise-plausible category headers.
- **Slides 11–14 (Team, Press, User Testimonials, Financial)** are absent from this particular file; their existence/order is confirmed via secondary sources, but verbatim text was not recovered.
- No slide, in this file or in five independent secondary breakdowns checked, contains explicit text about "trust," "risk," or "strangers" — the deck does not appear to have an explicit risk-disclosure slide on stranger trust; that theme is addressed only implicitly (via Market Validation's Couchsurfing/Craigslist adoption stats) according to analyst consensus, not stated outright by the founders in surviving deck text.
- businessinsider.com itself was blocked by the fetch tool and could not be reached directly this session.

## Honest Caveat

This is a third-party "reproduction"/teaching-template recreation of the deck, not the founders' original PowerPoint file — watermarked throughout as a PitchDeckCoach.com template.

Five slides' text is cross-verified as authentic across multiple independent sources. Two slides (Product, Competitive Advantages) contain visibly fabricated content — a modern post-2014-rebrand Airbnb UI and literal Lorem-ipsum placeholder text respectively — and must not be treated as original founder material.

Team, Press, User Testimonials, and Financial slide text was never recovered in verbatim form.

No source found contains explicit deck language about "trust between strangers" as a named risk; that framing (present in the user's original ask) does not appear to be supported by any surviving primary text — it is an analyst inference about implicit handling via the Market Validation slide, not a quote from the founders.
