# EBR Coworker — review copy for Meaghan

**From:** Beth Connor · **Date:** 2 Sep 2026 · **State:** review copy, not final (see "Where this is" below)

Three HTML files. Open them in a browser — no server, no build.

## What it is
A working concept prototype for the **EBR Coworker** — an AI coworker that does the prep for a client's quarterly business review (the pulls, the first draft) while the person keeps every judgment call. Part of a wider suite of coworkers.

## Reading order
1. **landing.html** — the vision/marketing page. Start here for the pitch: what the suite is and who it's for.
2. **ebr-app.html** — the actual product. Click through it: the coworker list, building the EBR coworker, its live risk-register dashboard. Left nav, tabs, toggles all respond.
3. **role-play-simulator.html** — an extended scenario. Linked from the app as "not MVP" — a fuller walk-through, not part of the core flow.

Every name, number, and grade in these files is **fictional** (made up for the demo). The coworker list and product model come from real N-able partner research.

## The "Bradley" panel
Both the landing page and the app have a **Bradley** panel (violet, on the right — a tab on the landing page, a button in the app's top bar). It's the honest read on the prototype: what's working, what will hurt us, and who needs to do what next. It is *not* part of the product — it's the review layer.
